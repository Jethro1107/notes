# Ludwig Angina

- **Definition** - aggressie, rapidly spreading "woody" or brawny cellulitis involving bilateral submandibular, sublingual and submental space
- **Mandibular anatomy** - comprised of multiple potential spaces inclduding 1) sublingual, 2) submandibular, and 3) submental spaces: 
![](~/Documents/notes/img/anatomy_of_potential_spaces_near_the_mandible.png)
- **Mechanism of spread of Ludwig angina:**
    - <u>Dental source of infection</u> - two-thirds of patients have dental source of infection involving 2nd-3rd mandibular molar teech, as these teeth are located **below the attachments of the myohyoid muscle**
    - <u>Other head and neck infections</u>:
        - Suppurative parotitis
        - Sialodenitis of the submandibular or sublingual salivary gland
        - Peritonsillar abscess
- **Etiological agent of Ludwig angina** - polymicrobial infections involving flora of the oral cavity:
    - <u>Gram positive cocci</u> - Viridans streptococci (S. anginosus, S. constellatus), S. aureus
    - <u>G- anaerobes</u> - Peptostreptococcus spp, Fusobacterium spp, Bacterioides
- **Clinical features of Ludwig angina** - note the <u>absence of lymphadenopathy</u> and <u>trismus</u> (unless paraparyngeal space involvement):
    - <u>Non-specific Mx</u> - **fever**, as well as fatigue, irritability, and decreased oral intake due to systemic upset and discomfort
    - <u>Mouth pain and odynophagia</u> - pain in the floor of mouth, often a/w **odynophagia**, **drooling**, and **'hot potato'** voice due to extreme pain on swallowing
    - <u>Stiff neck</u> - due to symmetrical woody induration in the submandibular region w/ associated crepetus
- **Signs in PTA** - note P/E should be performed in a setting where airway immediated protected by ET tube:
    - **General examination:**
        - <u>General apperance</u> - septic looking, anxious, drooling
        - <u>Posture</u> - sniffing position to open airway
        - <u>Respiration</u> - suprasternal retraction, rsepiratory distress +/- stridor
        - <u>Temperature</u> - pyrexic
    - **Examination of the oral cavity:**
        - <u>Tongue</u> - lingulal swelling
        - <u>Floor of mouth</u> - prominent edema of the floor of mouth (i.e. "double-tongue sign"), w/ tenderness 
        ![](~/Documents/notes/img/double_tongue_sign.png)
    - **Neck examination:**
        - <u>Chin/ submandibular region</u> - tender, symmetric, and "woody" induration occasionally w/ palpable crepetus (bull neck)
        - <u>LN</u> - characteristically **absence of LN**
- **Ix** - blood cultures and CT head and neck should be obtained
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Airway Mx</u> - fibreoptic intubation by nasal route is preferred
        - <u>Emprical IV broad-spectrum ABx</u> - both G+ aerobe, and G- anaerobe coverage
        - <u>Surgical Mx</u> - only if abscesses identified on CT
        - <u>Identification and Tx of source</u> - e.g. identification of an infected teeth
