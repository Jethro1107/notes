# Tension-type Headache

- **Definition** - most common type of primary headache syndrome a/w emotional and anxiety and is experienced to some degree by the majority of the population
- **Pathophysiology of TTH** - incompletely understood:
    - <u>Emotional precipitants</u> - emotions, stress or anxiety are common precipitants and there is sometimes an associated depressive episode
    - <u>Perpetuation of headache</u> - patients who notice the headache may become anxious about the headache, especially if convinced of a serious underlying conditions, and can lead to **continuation of Sx**
- **Clinical features of TTH:**
    - **Headache** - dull, tight or band-like pressure and generalised, usually radiating from the occipital region:
        - <u>Site</u> - generalised and **bilateral**, usually from the occipital region or vertex
        - <u>Onset</u> - gradual onset
        - <u>Progression</u> - attacks ranging from 30min to 7d, can remain unabated for weeks or months w/o interruption (cf migraines), usually less severe in the morning, but becomes troublesome as day goes on, and usually fluctuates w/ activity
        - <u>Quality</u> - dull, tight, band-like pressure
        - <u>Severity</u> - usually mild-to-moderate pain, fluctuates throughout the day
        - <u>Timing</u> - usually worse later in the day, but less noticeable when the patient is occupied by another activity (not exacerbated by physical activity)
    - **Absence of migraine-like features** - e.g. aura, vomiting, photophobia
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - **Abortive Tx** - analgesics play a role in aborting TTH attacks
        - **Prevention of next attack:**
            - <u>Patient education and re-assurance</u> - provided careful assessment followed by discussions of likely precipitates and reassurance that the prognosis is good and not caused by any organic disease
            - <u>Pharmacological therapy</u> - analgesics (limited benefits), low-dose amitriptyline
            - <u>Physiotherapy and stress management</u> - helps w/ muscle relaxation and stress management
