# Recurrent Urinary Tract Infections

- **Causes of recurrent infections:**
	- Relapse of a previously-treated infection
	- Re-infection by a different organism
- **Causes of chronic urinary tract infections:**
	- ABx resistance
	- Urological abnormalities serving as a foci of infection (e.g. urinary stones, chronically infected prostate), but usually in M and children, rather than F
- **Selection of ABx therapy for recurrent or chronic UTI:**
	- Augmentin 625 mg PO q12h for 10-14d
	- Trimethroprim 300 mg PO od for 10-14d
	- Cephalexin 500 mg PO q12h for 10-14d
	- Norfloxacin 400 mg PO q12h for 10-14d
- **Prevention of recurrent UTI:**
	- _Cranberries products_ - recent Cochrane Review (2023) demonstrates efficacy for prevention of recurrent symptomatic UTIs in adult F, but no evidence in children, and role as primary Tx of UTI
	- _ABx prophylaxis_ - can be given prn after intercourse of as a prolonged 3-6mo course:
		- Trimethroprin 150 mg PO nocte
		- Cephalexin 250 mg PO nocte
		- Norfloxacin 200-400 mg PO nocte (if demonstrated resistance)
