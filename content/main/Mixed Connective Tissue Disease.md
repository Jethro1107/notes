# Mixed Connective Tissue Disease

- **Definition** - CTD with features of SLE, systemic sclerosis, and myositis
- **Clinical features of MCTD:**
    - SLE component - symetrical non-erosive polyarthritis with synovitis over the hands
    - SSc component - Raynaud's phenomenon
    - Myositis component - muscle pain and weakness
- **Ix:**
    - Autoimmune markers - Anti-RNP +ve in most patients (but can be present in isolated SLE)
- **Mx** - manage individual components of the syndrome
