# Hepatomegaly

- **Definition** - ?liver span \> 11cm (along right MCL)
- **DDx of hepatomegaly:**
    - **Parenchymal hepatomegaly** - Alcoholic cirrhosis, Haemochromatosis
    - **Neoplastic hepatomegaly:**
        - Malignant hepatomegaly - Primary liver tumour (HCC, intrahepatic CC), secondary liver metastasis (GI primary and others)
        - Benign hepatomegaly - <u>focal nodular hyperplasia</u>, agiomyolipoma, hemangioma, adenoma
    - **Infiltrative hepatomegaly** - haematological malignancies (lymphoma, leukaemia, amyloidosis), sarcoidosis
    - **Vascular hepatomegaly** - RHF, TR, Budd-Chiari syndrome
    - **Cystic hepatomegaly** - Polycystic Kidney Disease, cystadenoma, <u>liver abscess</u>
- **Salient points of Hx:**
    - **HPI** - site, onset, progression, quality etc.
        - <u>Site</u> - is it truely hepatomegaly?
        - <u>Onset and progression</u> - since when? progressive?
    - **Associated Sx** - look for features suggestive of malignancy:
        - <u>Abdominal pain</u> - characterize site, onset, progression, and duration:
        - <u>Features of obstructive jaundice</u> (e.g. tea-coloured urine, pale stools, generalised pruritis) - suggestive of biliary obstruction
        - <u>Ascites</u> - any prior abdominal distension suggestive of HCC or peritoneal involvement
        - <u>Altered bowel habits</u> (changes in frequency, consistency, colour etc.)
        - <u>Constitutional Sx</u> - weight loss, anorexia
    - **PMH** - assessment of risk factors for cirrhosis and HCC:
        - <u>HBV/ HCV status</u> - ask about use of NRTIs, and control of HBV levels if known
        - <u>Alcoholic liver disease</u> - assessed through social Hx
        - <u>Other liver disease or cirrhosis</u> - cirrhosis is a risk factor of HCC irrespective of cause
        - <u>GI diseases</u> - e.g. inflammatory bowel disease, prior colonoscopy showing polyps
    - **SHx:**
        - <u>Smoking</u> - RF for upper GI malignancy
        - <u>Alcohol</u> - RF of alcoholic cirrhosis or HCC
        - <u>Diet</u> - RF for primary GI malignancy
    - **FHx** - FHx of HBV, liver maligancy, or GI malignancy
    - **Systems review** - Sx of other primary malignancies esp. if no known risk factors:
        - **Review of lower GI Sx** - points towards a primary CRC:
            - <u>Sx of left-sided CRC</u>:
                - PR bleeding
                - Obstructive Sx - e.g. constipation, partial obstruction resulting in colicky abdominal pain
                - Altered bowel habits - change in frequency, pencil-thin stool, alternating constipation and diarrhoea
            - <u>Sx of right-sided CRC</u>:
                - Abdominal mass
                - Anaemic Sx
            - <u>Sx of rectal cancer</u>:
                - Tenesmus
        - **Review of upper GI Sx** - points towards esophageal or gastric malignancy:
            - Epigastric discomfort/ Discomfort
            - Nausea and vomiting
            - UGIB
            - Dysphagia
- **P/E:**
    - **General examination** - pallor, jaundice, LN, <u>stigmata of chronic liver disease</u>
    - **Abdominal examination:**
        - <u>Inspection</u> - abdominal distension, visible mass, skin changes (e.g Sister Mary Joseph Nodule)
        - <u>Palpation</u> - characterise hepatomegaly or other organomegaly:
            - **Characterisation of hepatomegaly** - size, consistency, surface, edge, tenderness, pulsation, bruit:
                - <u>Diffuse smooth firm hepatomegaly</u> - suggestive of alcoholic cirrhosis or haematological malignancies
                - <u>Irregular firm or hard hepatomegaly</u> - HCC (can be quite soft as vascular), intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma, secondary malignancy
        - <u>Auscultation</u> - listen for **liver bruits**
        - <u>Test for shifting dullness</u> - any ascites
    - **PR bleeding** - rectal mass, PR bleeding etc.
    - **Examination of other Sx**
- **Ix:**
    - **Routine bloods** - CBC, LRFT, Clotting profile:
        - <u>CBC</u> - anaemia of chronic illness
        - <u>LFT</u> - dLFT vs cholestatic pattern
        - <u>Clotting profile</u> - prolonged PT suggestive of decompensated liver failure
    - **Viral hepatitis serology** - HBsAg, Anti-HBs, Anti-HBc, Anti-HCV
    - **Tumour markers** - AFP, CEA, CA19-9
    - **Imaging** - CXR, US upper abdomen, contrast CT T+A+P, staging imaging (contrast MRI, PET-CT)
    - **+/- other Ix** - based on clinical suspicion:
        - Endoscopy (if suspected primary GI) - colonoscopy (colorectal primary, +ve PR), upper endoscopy
        - Biopsy - FNAC, Trucut core biopsy (risk of bleeding, and tumour cell seeding)
- **Mx** - dependent on underlying cause
