# Alport's Syndrome

- **Definition** - glomerular disease caused by inherited abnormal collagen synthesis
- **Genetics of Alport's syndrome:**
    - <u>X-linked recessive</u> - inheritence of mutated or deleted COL4A5 gene on X chromosome (only M affected)
    - <u>Autosomal recessive</u> (rare) - inheritence of mutated COL4A3 or COL4A4 genes
- **Pathophysiology of Alport's syndrome:**
    - <u>Inheritence of mutated collagen gene in autosomal recessive fashion</u> - results in <u>accumulation of abnormal collagen</u> in the GBM, and cochlea
    - <u>Progressive degeneration of GBM</u> - affected patients progress from isolated glomerular haematuria to ESRD
- **Clinical features of Alport's syndrome:**
    - <u>Haematuria and renal impairment</u> - progression from isolated haematuria to ESRD in their late teens or twenties (F carriers of mutated COL4A5may develop haematuria but no significant renal disease)
    - <u>Sensorineural hearing loss</u> - due to degeneration of BM in the cochlea
- **Mx** - generally require RRT
