# Oliguria and Anuria

- **Definitions** - reduction of daily UO:
    - <u>Oliguria</u> - \< 300 ml/d
    - <u>Anuria</u> - \< 50 ml/d
- **DDx of oliguria/ anuria:**
    - Pre-renal AKI (shock)
    - Urinary tract obstruction (may manifest as AROU/CROU) - either distal to bladder neck, bilateral ureteric involvement, or unilateral in single-functioning kidney
- **Salient points of Hx:**
    - HPI - onset, associated Sx:
        - Associated Sx:
            - Sx of hypotension or volume depletion - points towards pre-renal AKI
            - Suprapubic pain exacerbation of fluid load - points towards AROU
- **P/E:**
    - Vitals - Hypotension/ shock
    - Abdomen - palpable bladder (AROU/CROU)
- **Ix:**
    - Routine bloods - CBC, RFT
    - Urinalysis - for protein and blood
    - Ultrasound - look for dilation of kidneys (may not be obvious in AKI due to lack of urine productions)
