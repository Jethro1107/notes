# Osteochondritis Dessicans

- **Definition** - segment of articular cartilage of femoral condyle undergoes necrosis and eventually separates t oform an intra-articular loose body
- **Etiology of osteochondritis dessicans** - different in juvenile and adult forms:
	- _Juvenile form_ - necrosis of a segment of articular artilage
	- _Adult form_ - detachment of a cyst of osteoarhtritic cartilage in a patient w/ Hx of OA (30% bilateral)
- **Epidemiology:**
	- _Juvenile form_ - most common in adolescent boys 5-15y
	- _Adult form_ - more common in M w/ KOA
- **Clinical features of osteochondritis dissecans** - dependent on presence of loose-body formation:
	- Knee pain
	- Knee effusion
	- Locking
- **Mx** - surgery if loose body +
