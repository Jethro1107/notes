# Walderstrom Macroglobulinaemia

- **Definition** - low-grade lymphoplasmacytoid lymphoma a/w an IgM paraprotein resulting in clinical features of hyperviscocity syndrome
- **Epidemiology:**
    - <u>Incidence</u> - rare tumour
    - <u>Demographic</u>:
        - Age - occurs only in the elderly
        - Sex - male preponderance
- **Clinical features of Waldenstrom macroglobulinaemia:**
    - **Features of hyperviscosity** - fatigue, headache, loss of concentration, epistaxis or bruising, amourosis fugax
    - **Features of underlying lymphoma:**
        - <u>Constitutional Sx</u> - fever, weight loss, night sweats
        - <u>Lymphadenopathy</u>
        - <u>Anaemia</u>
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Mx of hyperviscosity and anaemia</u> - plasmapheresis to remove IgM and make blood transfusion possible
        - <u>Systemic therapy</u> - Chlorambucil (alkylating agent), fludarabine, rituximab
