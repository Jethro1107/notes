# Generalised Musculoskeletal Pain

- **Definition** - non-specific S/S regarding pain that is often generalised (w/ or w/o a focal region), where clinical Hx and P/E can typically delineate the cause
- **Etiology of generalised musculoskeletal pain** - important to r/o <u>bone metastasis</u>:
    - <u>Malignant disease</u> - metastatic bone disease (particularly multiple myeloma)
    - <u>Disease of the bones</u> - osteomalacia, Paget's disease of the bone
    - <u>Disease of the joints</u> - osteoarthritis, joint hypermobility
    - <u>Rheumatological disease</u> - myositis, polymyalgia rheumatica

- **Ix:**
    - **Routine bloods** - CBC, LRFT, CaPO4, Vitamin D, PTH, plasma and urinary protein electrophoresis:
        - <u>CBC, LRFT</u> - baseline biochemistries (ALP may be elevated in Paget's disease)
        - <u>CaPO4, Vitamin D, PTH</u> - for detection of osteomalacia
        - <u>Plasma and urinary protein electrophoresis</u> - detection of multiple myeloma
    - **Imaging** - radionucleotide scanning, regional radiographs
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Dx and Tx of underlying cause</u> - consider specific Tx for known cause of pain
        - <u>Symptomatic relief</u> - analgesics or antineuropathic agents (e.g. amytryptiline, gabapentin)
