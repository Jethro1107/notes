# Haemophilia B

- **Definition** - congenital coagulation factor deficiency disorder characterised by deficiency of factor IX (Christmas Disease)
- **Clinical features** - clinically indistinguishable w/ haemophilia A, and frequence and severity depends on degree of deficiency
- **Mx** - similar as haemophilia A but replacement of factor IX concentrate (but rarely induce inhibitor Ab \[\<1%\], but may herard anaphylaxis)
