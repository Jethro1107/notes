# HSC Transplantation

- ****Terminology****
    - HSCT - Haematopoietic stem cell transplantation (encompasses BMT and PBSCT)
        - BMT - in the past, HSC can be only acquired through the marrow, hence called bone marrow transplant
        - PBSCT - Peripheral blood stem cell transplant, by mobilising HSC to circulation and extracting for transplantation
    - ****Types of HSCT**** - allogenic HSCT vs autologous HSCT
        - Allogenic HSCT - HSC comes from donor, either related (HLA-identical sibling), or closely-matched volunteer-unrelated donor (VUD)
        - Autologous HSCT - HSC harvested from patient and used when required
- ****Indications**** - being refined and extended (but primarily used for haematological disorders):
    - Neoplastic disorders of stem cell compartments - e.g. acute leukaemia (high risk at 1st remission or relapse), high risk MDS, CML (T315I, AP/BC), transformed MPN, relapsed lymphoma
    - Failure of haematopoiesis - e.g. aplastic anaemia, childhood BM failure syndrome (e.g. Fanconi's anaemia, Blackfan-Diamond, Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome)
    - Major inherited defects in blood cell production - Thalassemia major, sickle cell anaemia, SCID
    - Inborn errors of metabolism
- ****MOA:****
    - High-dose chemotherapy induces severe marrow failure and are at risk of complications.
    - Homing of allogenic HSC:
        - Replacement of diseased marrow with healthy marrow - haematological functions restored within 3-4 weeks (compared to several years)
        - Graft-versus-leukaemia effects - immunosurveillance of residual malignant cells and destruction (lower relapse rate)
- ****Procedure:****

    - Conditioning - chemotherapy +/- radiotherapy:
        - Additional eradication of residual leukaemia
        - Additional immunosuppression
        - Myeloablation - ablating recipient's haematopoietic tissue
    - Infusion of allogenic HSC cells - homing of donor cells in the marrow w/ a short cytopenic phase (2-3 days)
    - Immunosuppression - for GVHD prophylaxis
- ****Complications of HSCT:****
    - Complications related to conditioning - infections, haemorrhage, veno-occlusive disease of liver
    - Complications related to HSCT - graft rejection, GVHD
    - Late complications:
        - Cataract
        - Immunodeficiency
        - Endocrine dysfunction & fertility
        - Secondary malignancy
