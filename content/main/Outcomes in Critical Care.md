# Outcomes in Critical Care

- **Common measures of outcomes in ICU:**
    - ICU mortality - ~ 20% combined but variable
    - Hospital mortality - 30% combined
    - ICU length of stay
    - Hospital length of stay
- **Factors affecting ICU mortality:**
    - <u>Type of admission</u> - medical vs surgical
    - <u>Age</u> - single strongest independent risk factor of ICU mortality
    - <u>Comorbidities</u> - significant pre-existing comorbidities, esp. fever disease, severe cardiorespiratory disease, malignancy, immunosuppression, chronic renal failure
    - <u>General condition</u> - poor pre-existing nutritional state and/or general physical condition
    - <u>Acute disease severity</u> - e.g. APACHE score, SAPS score
    - <u>Disease course</u> - shock, respiratory failure, AKI etc.
- **Scoring systems:**
    - <u>Examples</u>:
        - APACHE score
        - SAPS II score
    - <u>Principle</u>:
        - Big data analysis - calculated from large databases generated from a range of ICUs in the States (principally used to evaluate performance relative to reference ICUs by calculating SMRs)
        - Predicted mortality - correlation with hospital mortality (but not 100% accurate)
