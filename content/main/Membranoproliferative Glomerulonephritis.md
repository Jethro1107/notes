# Membranoproliferative Glomerulonephritis

- **Definition** - also known as mesangiocapillary glomerulonephritis (MCGN), characterised by mesangial hypercellularity with thickening of glomerular capillary walls and submedothelial deposition of immune complexes +/- complements
- **Etiology of MPGN:**
    - Immune-complex subtype - chronic infections (hospital-acquired infections and IE), autoimmune diseases (cryoglobulinaemia), monoclonal gammopathy
    - Complement subtype - inherited or acquired complement types
    - Thrombotic microangiopathies - e.g. TTP, HUS (no immune deposits)
- **Clinical features of MPGN:**
    - Nephritic picture - haematuria and proteinuria
- **Mx:**
    - Ig subtype - Tx of underlying cause +/- immunosuppressants (e.g. MMF, cyclophosphamide)
    - Complement subtype - no specific treatment
