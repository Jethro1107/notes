# Stomas

- **Definition of stoma** - purposeful anastamosis between luminal organ and abdominal wall, where contents can be collected by external appliances
- **Features of stoma:**
    - Time course - may be temporary or permanent
    - Bowel involvement - character. content, position:
        - Ileostomy - sprouted, w/ liquid output, typically in right iliac fossa
        - Colostomy - flush, w/ semi-solid output, typically in left iliac fossa
- **Indications of stoma:**
    - **Feeding** (temporary):
        - <u>Reasons for indications</u> - enteral nutrition
        - <u>Choice of stoma</u> - percutaneous endoscopic gastrotomy, jejunostomy
    - **Decompression** (temporary):
        - <u>Reasons for indications</u> - prevent perforation as a result of proximal bowel dilatation from distal obstruction
        - <u>Choice of stoma</u> - loop ileostomy, loop colostomy
    - **Defunctioning** (temporary):
        - <u>Reasons for indications</u>:
            - Rest the bowel in severe ulcerative colitis
            - Reduce risk of anastamotic leaks post operatively
        - <u>Choice of stoma</u> - loop ileostomy, loop colostomy (or end- if distal portion perforated)
    - **Diversion** (permanent):
    - <u>Reasons for indications</u>:
        - Permanent faecal diversion after distal bowel resection (e.g. APR, Haartan's) or unresectable tumour
        - Urinary diversion after radical cystectomy (e.g. CA bladder)
    - <u>Choice of stoma</u>:
        - Permanent end ileostomy, permanent end colostomy
        - Urostomy by ileal conduit
- **Classification of stomas:**
- **Loop colostomy:**
    - Indications of loop colostomy - used to defunction a distal anstamosis after an anterior resection (less commonly employed and loop ileostomy now commonly performed)
    - Procedure - Mobilise loop of colon to surface, colostomy performed and edge of excision sutured to adjacent skin margin
    - Complications - prone to prolapse, and potential poor healing of anastamosis
- **End colostomy:**
    - Indications - after abdominoperineal excision of rectum (APR) or as part of Hartmann's procedure
    - Procedure - Bring divided colon through left iliac fossa trephine in rectus abdominis and skin, typicall at lateral edge of rectus sheath, above and medial to ASIS (prevents colostomy bag from impinging the ASIS)
- **Loop ileostomy:**
    - Indications - defunctioning of a low rectal anastamosis or an ileal pouch
    - Procedure - Mobilise a knuckle of ileum through skin trephine in right iliac fossa, w/ ileostomy incision, pulled over the top to more proximal part to create a spout
- **End ileostomy:**
    - Indications - temporarily in subtotal colectomy w/out anastamosis, or permanently in panproctocolectomy
    - Procedure - Mobilisation of ileum through rectus abdominis muscle, and formation of a spout
- **Complications of stoma:**
    - Skin irritation
    - Prolapse
    - Retraction
    - Ischaemia
    - Stenosis
    - Parastomal hernia
    - Bleeding
    - Fistulation

## Examination of the stoma

- **Aims of examination:**
    - Identify type of stoma and its indications - location, number of openings, calibre, sprouting, contents, and abdominal scars
    - Detection of complications
- **Inspection:**
    - <u>Location</u>:
        - Colostomy - LIF (end), epigastric (transverse loop)
        - Ileostomy - RIF
    - <u>Calibre</u>:
        - Colostomy - large
        - Ileostomy - small
    - <u>Number of openings</u>:
        - One opening - end-ostomy
        - Two adjacent openings - loop-ostomy
        - Two separate openings - double barrel stomas
    - <u>Sprouting</u>:
        - Flushed to skin - colostomy
        - Raised sprout (\> 3cm) - ileostomy or prolapsed colostomy
    - <u>Contents</u>:
        - Ileostomy - watery, billous output
        - Colostomy - firm frown faecal output
    - <u>Abdominal scars</u> - reveals underlying operation:
        - Midline laparotomy scar
        - APR scar
    - <u>Surrounding mucosa</u> - assessment of stoma necrosis:
        - Normal - pink and healthy
        - Ischaemic - dusky appearance
    - <u>Surrounding skin</u> - assessment of complications:
        - Erythema and excoriations - absence of dermatitis etc.
    - <u>Parastomal herniation</u> - ask patient to cough
- **Palpation:**
    - Recheck perastomal cough impulse
    - PR examination
