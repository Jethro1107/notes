# Shingles

- **Definition** - reactivation of latent VZV in a dermatomal pattern within dorsal root ganglion of sensory nerves after primary chickenpox
- **Clinical features of shingles** - multiple dermatomal reactivation may suggest underlying immune deficiency:
    - <u>Dermatomal discomfort</u> - burning sensation or parasthesia of the affected dermatome may occur 3-4d prior to the onset of characteristic lesions, and may be a/ transient viraemia
    - <u>Vesicular erruptions</u> - onset of characteristic rash progressing from macule, papule, pustule and subsequently crusted within 24h in a dermatomal pattern
    - <u>Satellite 'chickenpox' lesion</u> - transient viraemia may result in distant chickenpox lesions that do not reflect dermatomal reactivation
    - <u>Post-herpatic neuralgia</u> - troublesome persistence of pain for 1-6 mo following healing of rash which is more common with advanced age
- **Characteristic site of zoster reactivation:**
    - <u>Thoracic dermatome</u> - most typical manifestation due to reactivation from thoracic dorsal root ganglion resulting in a unilateral thoracic dermatomal rash
    - <u>Opthalmic division of trigeminal dermatome</u> - manifestation as **Herpes Zoster Opthalmicus** and constitutes an opthalmological emergency leading to blindness
    - <u>Facial nerve</u> - reactivation from geniculate ganglion manifests as **Ramsey Hunt Syndrome** w/ facial palsy, buccal ulceration, and rash in EAC
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Antiviral therapy</u> - demonstrated to be cost effective to reduce both early and late onset pain
        - <u>Tx of post-herpetic neuralgia</u> - aggressive analgesia for neuropathic pain may be required
    - **Tx of post-herpetic neuralgia:**
        - Amitryptyline 25-100 mg daily
        - Gabapentin (300 mg od or bid)
        - Capsaicin cream (0.0075%)
