# Neck Pain

- **Etiology of neck pain:**
    - **Mechanical neck pain:**
    - _Traumatic_ - whiplash injury, RTA
    - _Postural_ - torticollis, falling asleep in abnormal position
    - _Degenerative_ - cervical spondylosis, intervertebral disc herniation
    - **Non-mechanical neck pain:**
    - _Rheumatological disorders_ - RA, PMR, JIA, seronegative spondyloarthropathies, fibromyalgia
    - _Infections_ - septic arthritis, spondylitis, spondylodisciitis
    - _Metabolic disorders_ - osteoporosis, osteomalacia, paget's disease of the bone
    - _Neoplastic disorders_ - pancoast tumour, metastasis to the spine, multiple myeloma, lymphoma, intrathecal tumours
    - **Referred pain from distant sites:**
    - _Heart_ - angina pectoris typically lateralised in a dermatomal fashion (C3-4)
    - _Oral cavity_ - pharynx and dental problems
    - _Diaphragm_ - e.g. irritation of diaphragm but HBP pathologies
    - _Others_ - aortic aneurysm, cervical LN

- **Approach to clinical assessment:**
    - r/o traumatic causes (e.g. whiplash injury)
    - Screen for focal neurological S/S a/w cervical spondylosis
    - Screen for constitutional symptoms for sinister pathologies, e.g. fever (infections), weight loss (neoplasia)
    - r/o referred pain from the heart
- **Principles of Mx:**
    - _Orthotics_ - neck collars for injuries
    - _Pharmacological Tx_ - analgesics for symptomatic relief
    - _Investigations and referral_ - MRI for those w/ focal neurological S/S and referral
