# Familial Hypocalciuric Hypercalcaemia

- **Familial hypocalciuric hypercalcaemia** (FHH) - an autosomal dominant disorder resulting in poor functioning of the calcium sensing receptors (CaSR)
- **Pathophysiology of FHH:**
    - Normal physiology of PTH secretion:
        - Negative regulation by Ca levels - High Ca levels detected by CaSR promotes PTH degradations in the chief cells, inhibiting Ca release.
    - Decreased sensitivity of CaSR results in PTH secretion despite high-normal-to-elevated levels of Ca -\> inappropriate normal to elevated PTH
    - Increased Ca2+ reabsorption in the kidneys, resulting in hypocalciuria.
- **Clinical features of FHH:**
    - Almost always asymptomatic and complications never occur.
    - Iatrogenesis if misdiagnosed as primary hyperPTH and subjected to unnecessary investigations and management.
- **Dx:**
    - Hypercalcaemia
    - High PTH
    - Low 24h Ca/Cr ratio
