# Osgood-Schlatter Disorder

- **Definition** - traction apophysitis resulting from repetitive traction stresses at the insertion of the patellar tendon onto the tibial tuberosity
- **Epidemiology** - common cause of knee pain n children:
	- _Prevalence_ - common in those exposed to tractional sterss (e.g. sports w/ running, kicking, and rumping)
	- _Demographic_:
		- Age - most common in 10-14y
		- Sex - male preponderance (3:1)
- **Clinical features** - bilateral onset in 33% of cases:
	- **Knee pain** - loaclised pain at the tibial tuberosity:
		- _Site_ - localised to tibial tubersoty
		- _Onset_ - insiduous, common in childrenplaying sports, typically lasts 12 mo
		- _Provocation_:
			- Physical activity - during and after activity
			- Stair climbing - aggravated by walking up and down stairs
			- Kneeling - worse w/ kneeling (local tenderness)
		- ![[Pasted image 20260524160031.png]]
	- **Deformities** - localised swelling +/- lump formation in the affected tibial tuberosity
- **Signs:**
	- Localised swelling and tenderness of affected tubercle
	- Pain reproduced by attempts to straighten flexed knee against resistance (i.e. resisted extension of affected knee)
- **Ix** - X-ray:
	- Widening of apophysis w/ possible fragmentation of bone
	- Exclude tumours or fractures
- **Mx:**
	- **Principles of Mx:**
		- _Reassurance_ - reassure self-limiting condition
		- _Advice_:
			- Abstainence or modification of active sports
			- Ice packs after physical activities
			- Quadriceps strengthening
			- Graded return to full activity
		- _Analgesics_ - may be offered
		- _Prevention_ - program of stretching exercise for quadriceps mechanism in children in sport
