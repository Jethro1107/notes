# Asymptomatic Bacteriuria

- **Definition** - presence of \> 10<sup>5</sup> organisms / mL in urine in an otherwise healthy individuals
- **Epidemiology** - 30% will develop symptoms after 1y:
    - <u>Prevalence</u>:
        - Children \< 1y - 1%
        - Schoolgirls - 1%
        - Schoolboys - 0.03%
        - Men - 1%
        - Non-pregnant women - 3%
        - Pregnant women - 5%
        - Long-term catheterisation - 100% have florid G- bacteria
- **Indications of Tx:**
	- Infants
	- Pregnancy (increased risk of ascending infection and obstetrics complication)
	- Urological abnormalities
	- Pending urological procedures where mucosal bleeding is anticipated
