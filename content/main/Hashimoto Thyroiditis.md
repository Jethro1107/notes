# Hashimoto Thyroiditis

- **Definition** - an autoimmune thyroid disease characterised by destructive lymphoid infiltration, ultimately leading to varying degree of fibrosis and thyroid enlargement
- **Terminology** - confusing terminology for 'autoimmune hypothyroidism', but are likely similar disease w/ same Mx:
    - <u>Hashimoto's thyroiditis</u> - some only refer to those with a **firm goitre** with +ve Anti-TPO, irrespective of thyroid status (may be euthyroid and hypothyroid)
    - <u>Spontaneous atrophic hypothyroidism</u> - refer to those **w/o a goitre**, in whom TSH-receptor blocking Ab plays a greater role in pathogenesis than Anti-TPO (atrophic goitre despite physiological increase in TSH since hypothyroid reflects inhibitory Anti-TSH Ab role in pathogenesis)
- **Epidemiology:**
    - <u>Incidence</u> - 3.5/1000/y in F, 0.8/1000/y in M
    - <u>Demographic</u> - increasing incidence w/ age, F preponderance
- **Clinical features of hashimoto thyroiditis:**
    - **Diffuse goitre** (may be only complaint) - usually small to medium sized goitre, clinically indistinguishable from a simple goitre, that has a <u>firm or rubbery</u> texture characteristically
    - **Hypothyroidism** (25%) - only a quarter of patients are hypothyroid at presentation, most present at euthyroid (but TSH raised), and rarely in initial hyperthyroid phase:
        - <u>Sx of hypothyroidism</u> - cold intolerance, weight gain, dry skin, fatigue
        - <u>Signs of hypothyroidism</u> - bradycardia, myxoedema, hoarseness etc.
- **Ix:**
    - **TFT** - only 25% are biochemically hypothyroid:
        - <u>Hypothyroidism</u> (25%) - low T4, raised TSH
        - <u>Subclinical hypothyroidism</u> (75%) - normal T4, high-normal/ raised TSH (at risk of overt hypothyroidism in the long run)
    - **Thyroid antibodies:**
        - <u>Antithyroid peroxidase</u> (Anti-TPO) - +ve in 90% of patient's w/ hashimoto thyroiditis
        - <u>Anti-thyroglobulin</u> (Anti-TG) - may be +ve
    - **ANA** - may be +ve if presenting in young patients (e.g. \< 20y)
- **Mx:**
    - **Levothyroxine replacement therapy** - see details in notes on hypothyroidism:
        - As Tx of overt hypothyroidism to restore euthyroid status
        - To shrink goitre by sufficiently suppress serum TSH to a low but detectable level
