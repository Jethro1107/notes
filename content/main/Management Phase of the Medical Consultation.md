# Management Phase of the Medical Consultation

- **Sequence of the Mx:**
    
    ![[Pasted image 20260414192123.png]]
    - <u>Diagnosis</u> - tell patient the diagnosis and potential differentials, if not possible, describe the problem as it relates to the presenting Sx
    - <u>Illness education</u> - establish clear-cut baseline of information to ensure patient understands the need to treat (includes correcting false beliefs)
    - <u>Development of Mx plan</u>:
        - **Time-sensitive** - immediate (always included even if no action is proposed), long term, preventive
        - **Nature** - pharmacological, non-pharmacological, lifestyle modification
    - <u>Explore other alternatives</u> - if applicable explore other preventive opportunities
    - <u>Evaluate the consultation</u> - establish whether the objectives of both have been met and the patient is happy with the outcome
    - <u>Arrange follow-up</u> - if long-term review need to be made
- **Patient management strategy** (RAPRIOP) - what are the actionable items in patient management:
    - <u>Reassurance, education</u> - reassurance as appropriate based on substantial foundation, and education on the likely causes
    - _Advice_ - offer concrete action that the patient may take
    - <u>Prescription</u> - requires strong clinical knowledge
    - <u>Investigations</u> - as needed (see above)
    - <u>Referral</u> - balance and refer appropriately (gatekeeper role)
    - <u>Observation and follow up</u> - time as a diagnostic marker
    - <u>Prevention</u> - primary, secondary or tertiary prevention
- **Prescriptions** - rational prescribing, considering the following; if in doubt whether or not to give a drug, don't:
	- Patient's expectations
	- Knowledge of the disease
	- Knowledge of the drug, including drug interactions, and adverse effects
- **Referral:**
	- Referral to secondary and tertiary care
	- Referral to paramedical services, such as PT, ST, social workers
- **The healing art of the doctor through counselling** - Balint terms this the therapeutic effects of the doctor:
	- Therapeutic effect of the doctor during counselling has been _well documented_ in general practice
	- Highly dependent on if the doctor has a _certain professional charisma, and is caring and competent_
	- Patients may _benefit variably to counselling_, but we cannot underestimate the dependence of patients on the healing factor, especially when significant psychic factors in play
