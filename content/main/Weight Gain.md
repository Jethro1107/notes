# Weight Gain

- **Key facts and figures:**
    - The cause of exogenous obesity is multifactorial
    - Abdominal obesity gives higher cardiovascular risk at any weight
    - Central obesity is a major risk factor of insulin resistance, T2DM, and other manifestations of metabolic syndrome
    - Even small weight losses are effective in preventing DM and cardiovascular risk profile
    - The onset of obesity can occur at any age
    - Secondary or pathologiical causes are rare - \< 1% of obese patients have an identifiable secondary cause of obesity
    - Only two conditions can be identified clinically - Cushing's syndrome, and hypothyroidism
- **Murtagh's diagnostic approach to weight gain:** 
    - 
    ![](img/murtagh_diagnostic_approach_to_weight_gain.png)
	- **Probability diagnosis:**
		- <u>Exogenous obesity</u> - excessive caloric intake coupled with lack of exercise largely determined by environmental influences with some considerable genetic influence
	- **Serious causes not to be missed:**
		- <u>Hypothalamic disorders</u>:
			- Structural hypothalamic disorders (often a/w other features of hypopituitarism, headaches and VF defects) - e.g craniopharyngioma, optic gliomas, and pituitary neoplasms
			- Encephalitis
		- <u>Disorders related to Na and water retention</u> - weight gain is attributed to increased water weight:
			- Congestive heart failure
			- Liver failure, cirrhosis and portal hypertension
			- Nephrotic syndrome
			- Chronic kidney disease
	- **Pitfalls:**
		- <u>Pregnancy</u> - may be presenting early in unknown pregnancy, or post-partum as inability to return to prepartum energy requirements
		- <u>Endocrine disorders</u>:
			- **Hypothyroidism** - characterised by weight gain despite poor appetite, cold intolerance, dry skin, and non-specific fatigue
			- **Cushing's syndrome** - may present w/ weight gain a/w cushingoid changes, but presentation is variable dependent on underlying cause (e.g. ectopic ACTH production a/w weight loss as effects of cachexia predmominates)
			- **Insulinoma** - very rare disease, but can be identified by presence of both 1) hypoglycaemia, and 2) obesity
			- **Polycystic ovarian disease** - especially in women
		- <u>Congenital abnormalities</u>:
			- Klinefelter syndrome (XXY karyotype)
			- Turner syndrome (XO karyotype)
			- Rare congenital disorders - e.g. Prader-Willi syndrome, Laurence-Moon-Biedl syndrome
	- **Seven masquerades list** - depression, drugs, thyroid disorders
	- **Psychogenic considerations** - reason for consultation should be explored
- **Salient points of Hx:**
    - **HPI** - onset, duration, quantification, association w/ diet, appetite and physical activity:
        - <u>Onset and duration</u> - when has recent weight changes started?
        - <u>Quantification</u> - Exactly how much weight is gained and for how long?
        - <u>Associations</u>:
            - Diet - requires a careful dietary Hx as some deny overeating or underestimate their food intake
            - Appetite - change in appetite is a/w pathological causes (e.g. hypothyroidism)
            - Physical activity - quantify level of physical activity
    - **Careful dietary Hx:**
        - Tell me in detail what you ate yesterday
        - Whats a normal meal for you like?
        - Tell me about snakes, soft drinks and alcohol
    - **Associated Sx:**
        - <u>Cold intolerance and dry skin</u> - points towards hypothyroidism
        - <u>Recent HTN</u> - new onset HTN a/w Cushing's syndrome
        - <u>LL swelling</u> - a/w disorders related to Na and water retention
    - **PMH:**
        - Assesssment of cardiovascular risk - HTN, DM, dyslipidaemia, smoking Hx
        - Drug Hx
    - **Relavent FHx and SHx**
- **P/E:**
    - <u>Measurement of body parameters</u> - body weight, height (BMI), and waist circumference
    - <u>BP measurements</u> - new onset hypertension suggestive of Cushing's
    - <u>General examination</u> - look for features of Cushing's syndrome, acromegaly and hypothyroidim, and search for features suggestive of DM, dyslipidaemia and alcohol abuse
- **Ix:**
    - **Routine bloods** - LRFT, Fasting blood glucose, lipid profile +/- TFT and cortisol
    - **ECG and CXR** - if \> 40y for suspected cardiovascular disease
