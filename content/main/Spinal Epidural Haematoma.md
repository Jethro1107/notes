# Spinal Epidural Haematoma

- **Definition** - haemorrhage into the epidural (rarely subdural) spaces resulting in acute focal or radicular pain followed by variable evidence of spinal cord or conus medullaris disorders
- **Risk factors of spinal epidural hematoma:**
    - <u>Traumatic</u> - trauma to the spine
    - <u>Bleeding tendancies</u> - therapeutic anticoagulation, tumors, haematological malignancies
    - <u>Iatrogenic</u> (exceedingly rare, negligible) - LP, epidural anaesthesia
- **Clinical features of spinal epidural hematoma** - acute serial evolution of Sx w/ known risk factors:
    - <u>Pain</u> - acute focal or radicular pain initially
    - <u>Features of cord compression</u>:
        - Evidence of extradural spinal cord compression
        - Occasionally presents w/ Conus medullaris syndrome w/ relatively spared LL motor functions (absence of hemiplegia)
- **Ix** - MRI or CT to delineate extent of bleeding (LP avoided)
- **Mx:**
    - Reversal of underlying clotting disorder
    - Surgical decompression
