# Acute Colonic Pseudo-obstruction

- **Definition** - also known as Ogilvie's syndrome, characterised by sudden onset of painless, massive enlargement of the proximal colon in the absence of features of mechanical obstruction
- **Demographic** - a/w bed-ridden patients w/ severe extracolonic disease or trauma
- **Clinical features** - painless distended abdomen

- **Complications of acute colonic pseudo-obstruction:**
    - Perforation
    - Peritonitis
- **Ix:**
    - _AXR_ - colonic dilatation w/ air extending into rectum (i.e. no distal collapse), caecal diameter > 10 mm predicts perforation
    - _Barium enema_ - no mechanical obstruction
- **Dx** - Dx by exclusion of mechanical IO
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
    - _Dx and Mx of underlying cause_ - e.g. correction of any biochemical abnormalities
    - _AChE inhibitors_ - neostigmine effective for enhancing parasympathetic activity and gut motility
    - _Endoscopic Mx_ - decompression w/ rectal tube or by colonoscopy effective but may need to be repeated
    - _Stoma creation_ - defunctioning caecostomy by surgical or fluoroscopic means
