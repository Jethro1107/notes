# Renal Infection

- **Definition** - infection of the renal parenchyma arising acquired in the following ways:
    - <u>Haematogenous spread from primary site</u> - tonsils, dentals, or cutaneous infections (esp. boils and carbuncles), +/- renal TB from chest, LN
    - <u>Ascending infection</u> (more common) - from the urniary tract, especially when there is vesicoureteric reflux, urinary stasis, and presence of calculi
- **Types of kidney infections:**
    - Acute pyelonephritis - in childhood, pregnancy and with urinary obstruction
    - Chronic pyelonephritis - reflux nephropathy
    - Pyonephrosis
    - Renal abscess
    - Perinephric abscess
    - Renal tuberculosis
- **Bacteriology** - dependent on source of infections:
    - Ascending infections - E. coli and other G- bacteria
    - Haematogenous infections - Streptococcus, Staphylococcus
    - Differentiation by urine pH:
        - Acidic urine (low urine pH) - E. coli, streptococcus
        - Alkali urine (high urine pH) - Proteus spp, staphylococcus (urease-producing), predisposes to stone formation

## Acute pyelonephritis

- **Epidemiology:**
    - Demographic - F \> M, during childhood, at puberty, after intercourse, during pregnancy and during menopause
- **Clinical features of acute pyelonephritis** - triad of loin pain, fever and tenderness over kidneys:
    - <u>Prodrome</u> - headache, lassitude, and nausea
    - <u>Acute loin pain</u> - sudden onset of pain in the flanks and hypochondrium
    - <u>Features of septicaemia</u> - remitting high fever (\> 39 degrees), chills and rigors
    - <u>Features of cystitis</u> - urgency, frequency, scalding dysuria
    - <u>Features of severe cases</u>:
        - Severe fever (\> 40 degrees) often without corresponding tachycardia
        - N/V
        - Sweating
        - Extremely unwell
- **DDx of acute pyelonephritis:**
    - Pneumonia
    - Causes of acute abdomen - appendicitis, cholecystitis
- **Mx:**
    - Pain relief - NSAID +/- opiods
    - Rehydration - drink water copiously +/- IV fluid replacement
    - Empirical broad spectrum ABx - e.g. amoxicillin\* Neoplasms of the kidney

## Chronic pyelonephritis

- **Epidemiology:**
    - Demographic - F \> M, earlier diagnosis in F:
        - 2/3 of F diagnosed \< 40y
        - 60% of M diagnosed \> 40y
- **Definition** - also known as 'reflux nephropathy'. chronic ascending infection of the kidneys typically a/w vesicoureteric reflux, and is an important cause of end-stage renal disease (ESRD)
- **Pathology** - interstitial inflammation and fibrosis of the renal parenchyma in a <u>patchy distribution</u>
- **Clinical features:**
    - Asymptomatic until advanced renal insufficiency - unusual but possible
    - **Lumbar pain** - dull and non-specific in character
    - **Features of UTI** - dysuria, and urinary frequency is common
    - **Features of progressive renal insufficiency:**
        - Non-specific Sx - tiredness, malaise, anorexia, nausea, headaches
        - HTN (40%) - can manifest as <u>malignant HTN</u>
        - NcNc anaemia - can be presenting Sx
- **Mx:**
    - Eradication of predisposing factors - relieving obstruction
    - Treating infections - often require multiple courses of ABx +/- nephrectomy (if confined to 1 kidney)
    - Managing CKD - will eventually require RR

## Pyonephrosis

- **Definition** - infection of a hydronephrosis, often a complication of renal stone disease or acute pyelonephritis
- **Etiology** - renal stone disease
- **Clinical features** - triad of anaemia, fever and swelling in the loin:
    - <u>Loin swelling</u> - accumulation of pus may result in a ballotable kidney
    - <u>Anaemia</u> - worsening SOB
    - <u>Fever</u> - often extremely high and a/w rigors (impending septicaemia)
    - <u>Sx of cystitis</u> (can predominate) - dysuria, frequency, urgency
- **Mx** - surgical emergency due to impending lethal sepsis and permanent renal damage:
    - IV ABx
    - Drainage of kidney - percutaneous or open nephrostomy
    - Removal of stone (if present)
    - Nephrectomy - considered if function of contralteral kidney preserved
