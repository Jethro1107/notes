#### Alzheimer's Disease

- **Definition** - neurodegenerative disorder a/w amyloid deposition and is the <u>most common cause of dementia</u>
- **Epidemiology:**
    - Most common cause of Dementia
    - Rare under 45y
- **Etiology of AD:**
    - **Genetic factor** - 15% cases are familial
    - <u>Early onset AD</u> - usually single gene w/ autosomal dominant inheritance (e.g. apoeprotein E4)
    - <u>Late onset AD</u> - inheritance is likely polygenic
- **Pathophysiology of AD:**
    - <u>Deposition of amyloid plaques</u> - IHC staining demonstrates significant quantities of amyloid in the plaques, organised as senile plaques and neurofibrillary tangles
    - <u>Neurotransmitter abnormalities</u> - impairment of cholinergic transmission, while abnormalities of noradrenaline, 5-HT, glutamate and substance P
    - <u>Cerebral atrophy</u> - macroscopic atrophy occurs particularly in the cerebral cortex and the hippocampus
- **Clinical features of AD** - gradual progression with worsening w/ intercurrent physical illness:
    - <u>Inability to remember new information</u> - due to hippocampal atrophy resulting in gradual impairment of memory
    - <u>Other cortical functions</u> - e.g. apraxia, visuo-spatial impairment and aphasia, anosognia (denying cognitive impairment)
    - <u>Depression</u> - often common
