# Churg-Strauss Syndrome

- **Definition** - currently known as eosinophilc granulomatosis with polyangiitis (EGPA) small vessel vasculitis a/w eosinophilia and asthma with multisystem involvement
- **Epidemiology:**
    - <u>Incidence</u> - 1-3/1000000/y
- **Clinical features of Churg-Strauss syndrome** - acute presentation classically with triad of 1) skin lesions, 2) assymetrial mononeuritis multiplex, and 3) eosinophilia:
    - **Prodromal period** - may last for many years characterised by:
        - Allergic rhinitis
        - Recurrent nasal polyps
        - Poorly-controlled late-onset asthma
    - **Small airway and pulmonary disease** - occurs prior to and during active vasculitic phase of disease:
        - <u>Asthma</u> - cardinal feature of EGPA, often late-onset asthma that is difficult to control that is present preceding the Dx
        - <u>Pulmonary infiltrates</u> - eosinophilic pulmonary infiltrates may be seen on CXR
        - <u>Pleural effusion</u> - eosinophilic serositis may be present
    - **Upper airway disease** - upper airway involvement is often present prior to Dx, and clinically mimics aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease (Samter's triad) in absence of eosinophilia:
        - <u>Allergic rhinitis</u> - characterised by nasal congestion and discharge
        - <u>Recurrent nasal polyposis</u> - causing nasal obstruction
    - **Skin lesions** - most common feature of the vasculitic phase:
        - <u>Palpable purpura</u> - as a result of a vasculitic phenomenon
        - <u>Nodules</u> - tender subcutaneous nodules on extensor surfaces of extremities reflecting eosinophilic granulomas
    - **Asymmetrical mononeuritis multiplex** (75%) - sensory and motor peripheral neuropathy at presentation which may progress into a polyneuropathy if left untreated
    - **Mesenteric vasculitis** (50%) - Abdominal Sx may be present suggesting mesenteric vasculitis
- **Ix:**
    - **Routine bloods** - CBC, ESR/CRP:
        - <u>CBC</u> - eosinophilia during active disease
        - <u>ESR/CRP</u> - non-specifically raised
    - **Autoantibodies** - may have +ve MPO or PR3 but should be considered a <u>distinct entity from ANCA-associated vascuitides</u>
    - **Bx of affected site:**
        - Evidence of small-vessel vasculitides
        - Eosinophilic infiltration of the vessel wall
- **Mx:**
    - <u>Induction therapy</u> - high-dose steroid + cyclophosphamide
    - <u>Maintenance therapy</u> - low-dose steroid and azathioprine, methotrexate or MMF
