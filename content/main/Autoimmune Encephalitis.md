# Autoimmune Encephalitis

- **Definition** - inflammation of the brain parenchyma mimicking viral encephalitis, a/w underlying malignancies and is not of infective etiology
- **Epidemiology** - rare disorder, most commonly affecting young women w/ ovarian teratomas or children
- **Clinical features:**
    - Viral like prodrome (fever)
    - Neuropsychiatric manifestations
    - Abnormal movements
    - Seizures
    - Altered GC
- **Ix** - anti-NMDA receptors
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>General measures</u> - supportive care, close monitoring +/- ASM
        - <u>Immunosuppression</u> - high dose IV steroids, IVIG or plasma-exchange
