# Anal Fissure

- **Definition** - a superficial mucosal tear in the anoderm at the dentate line, commonly in the posterior midline
- **Site of anal fissures:**
    - <u>Posterior midline</u> - classical position occurs in 90% of cases
    - <u>Anterior midline</u> - rarer but occurs in 10% of F w/ anal fissures
    - <u>Atypical position +/- multiple fissures</u> - caused by atypical etiology (e.g. IBD, TB, syphilis, HIV, CMV)
- **Etiology of anal fissures:**
    - <u>Traumatic damage over anal mucosa</u> - e.g. constipation, hard stools, internal sphincter spasm
    - <u>Ischaemic damage</u> - ischaemia over the posterior midline resulting in superficial mucosal tear
    - <u>Atypical etiologies</u> - causing multiple fissures in atypical sites:
        - **Inflammatory bowel disease**
        - **Infections** - e.g. TB, Syphilis, HIV, CMV
- **Clinical features of anal fissures:**
    - **Anal pain** - exacerbated by <u>daefacation</u> or <u>spasms of internal anal sphincter</u>
    - **PR bleeding** - minor fresh rectal bleeding in association w/ defaecation
    - **Features suggestive of chronic fissures:**
        - <u>Sentinel piles</u> - oedematous skin tag adjacent to the fissure may be present
        - <u>Hypertrophic papilla</u> - small raised skin tags from the dentate line that is responsible for causing **pruritis**
        - <u>Internal sphincter muscle at base of sphincter</u>
- **Clinical assessment and Dx** - based on perianal examination (DRE and proctoscopy are not indicated as extremely painful)
- **Mx:**
    - **Medical Tx** - avoid hard stools and constipation, and reduce internal sphincter tone:
        - <u>Avoidance of constipation</u> - Bulk-forming laxatives, stool softeners w/ adequate fluid intake
        - <u>Reducted internal sphincter pressure</u> - topical nitroglycerin, diltiazem, or butulinum toxin injection
    - **Surgical Tx** - lateral internal sphincterotomy:
        - <u>Clinical efficacy</u> - 95% healing rate
        - <u>Complications</u>:
            - Faecal incontinence (0-15%) - most are minor flatus incontinence
