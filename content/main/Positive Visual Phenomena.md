# Positive Visual Phenomena

- **Types of positive visual phenomena:**
    - <u>Fortification spectra</u> - often described as silvery zigzag lines
    - <u>Teichopsia</u> - flashing couloured light
    - <u>Phosphenes</u> - simple flashes of light
    - <u>Halos</u> - bright circular rings around light as a result of glaucoma
    - <u>Visual hallucinations</u> - for complex formed visual elements
- **DDx of of positive visual phenomena:**
    - <u>Migraine</u> (most common) - classically w/ fortification spectra or teichopsia preceeding the headache
    - <u>Retinal pathology</u> (e.g. detachment) - many manifest as phosphenes which is mononuclear
    - <u>Primary cortex pathology</u> - may manifests as phosphenes to one side of midline
