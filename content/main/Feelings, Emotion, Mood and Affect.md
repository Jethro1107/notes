# Feeling, Emotion, Mood and Affect

- **Definition and terminology** - customary to distinguish between feeling and emotions:
    - <u>Feeling</u> - defined as a +ve or -ve reaction to some experience or event and is the subjective experience to emotion
    - <u>Emotion</u> - a stirred-up state often accompanied by physiological changes occuring in a response of some event, and which tends to maintain or abolish the causative event
    - <u>Mood</u> - a pervasive or sustained emotion that colours the person's perception of the world
    - <u>Affect</u> - short-lived emotion, and is defined as the patient's emotional responsiveness inferred by the doctor from the patient's body language and facial expression (described in terms of congruence w/ mood)
- **Descriptors of mood:**
    - Intensity
    - Duration
    - Fluctuations
    - Adjectival description of the type
- **Affect:**
    - <u>Descriptors</u> - ?labile, within normal range, constricted, blunt or flat
    - <u>Congruence</u>:
        - Congruence w/ mood
        - Incongruence w/ mood
- **5 levels of emotional reaction and expression of clinical relevance:**
    - <u>Normal emotional reactions</u> - emotional states that are the result of events and that lie within cultural and social norms
    - <u>Abnormal emotional reactions</u> - emotional states that are understandable but excessive
    - <u>Abnormal emotional expressions</u> - emotional expressions that are very different from average normal reaction where the person is usually aware of the abnormality
    - <u>Morbid disorders of emotional expression</u> - abnormal emotional expression where the person is unaware of the abnormality
    - <u>Morbid disorders of emotion</u> - pathological states which, although triggered by stressful events, do not spontaneously resolve with the removal of the stressor and therefore have independent momentum
