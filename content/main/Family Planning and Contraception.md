# Family Planning and Contraception

- **Domains of a family planning consultation** - it often begins w/ the presentation of a young woman for contraceptive advice:
	- Addressing health concerns
	- Health promotion
	- Menses regulation
	- Discussions regarding health-related aspects of sexual activity
	- Planned parenthood
	- Fertility and infertility
	- Pregnancy prevention
	- STI prevention
	- Immunisation (HPV)
	- Cervical cancer screening
- **Important considerations for a family planning consultation:**
	- Initial visit is critical and an excellent opportunity to develop good rapport to ensure patient comes back for advice regarding family planning
	- Confidentiality of patients,  especially for teenagers, keeping in mind of Gillick test of competency
	- Figures and aids recommended for education to enhance therapeutic relationship and facilitate compliance
- **Factors influencing choice of contraception:**
	- Individual needs and personal preference
	- Effectiveness
	- Safety and incidence of S/E
	- Availability
- **Most commonly used contraception in order of preference in the Western World:**
	- Male condom
	- Combined oral contraceptive (COC) pills
	- Intra-uterine device (IUD)
	- Female sterilisation
	- Withdrawal
- **Long-acting reversible contraception** (LARC) - non-permanent contraception administered less frequently than once a month, and are most effective reversible contraceptives (failure rates of typical use same as for perfect use):
	- Implants
	- IUDs
	- Injectables

- **Contraceptive failure** - 50% of pregnancies are unintended in the US and occur because:
	- Non-use of contraception
	- Failure of a specific contraceptive method
	- Discontinuation of contraceptive or barrier methods
- **Dual protection:**
	- Use of condoms in addition to an effective form of contraception
	- Offers both STI (only offered by condoms) and pregnancy prevention

- **Medical Elligibility Criteria tables for contraceptive use** (MEC) - an internationally recognised system to guide clinicians in the safe provision of contraceptive methods, classifying conditions affecting eligibility under 4 categories:
	- _MEC 1_ - no restrictions
	- _MEC 2_ - the benefits generally outweigh the risks
	- _MEC 3_ - the risks generally outweigh the the benefits
	- _MEC 4_ - absolute contraindications
