# Faecal Impaction

- **Definition** - large, hard mass of stool fills the rectum, commonly affecting institutionalised patients
- **Contributing factors of faecal impaction:**
    - _Premorbid status_ - frail elderly, those w/ dementia, or disabled, immobile patients
    - _Constipating drugs_ - e.g. verapamil, opioids, drugs w/ anticholinergic effects
    - _Autonomic neuropathy_ - e.g. severe DM resulting in altered colonic activity
    - _Painful anal conditions_ - e.g. anal fissures, haemorrhoids
- - **Clinical features of faecal impaction** - typically presents not w/ constipation, but w/ other abdominal or systemic S/S:
    - **Abdominal Sx:**
    - _Abdominal pain_ - lower Abd pain w/ colicky or bloating sensation
    - _Constipation_ - usually unaware of the problem, as typically presents as frequent amounts of small stools +/- sense of inadequate defecation
    - _Diarrhoea_ - spurious diarrhoea usually the main complaint due to bacterial liquefaction of faeces, ocassionally resulting in **faecal incontinence**
    - **Systemic manifestations** - e.g. malaise, anorexia, nausea, confusion, headaches
- **Complications of faecal impaction:**
    - Spurious diarrhoea and faecal incontenence
    - Intestinal obstruction
    - Megacolon and perforation
    - Bleeding
    - Urinary incontinence or retention
    - Urinary tract infection
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
    - _General measures_ - adequate hydration and avoiding laxatives
    - _Addressing contributing factors_ - e.g. consider Tx of polypharmacy
    - _Pharmacological Tx_ - osmotic laxatives (e.g. 8 sachets of macrogol 3350 x 3d, fleet enema)
    - _Digital disimpaction_ - often requires softening impacted stool by faecal softeners (e.g. arachis oil enema) and careful digital removal
