# The Medical Consultation

- **Definition of medical consultation** (Sir James Spence, 1960):
    - A formalised interaction between doctor and patient in settings that may vary from a clearly defining task such as suturing a simple wound to the complexities of vague undifferentiated illness with profound psychological issues
    - The essential unit of medical practice is the occasion when in the imtimacy of the consulting room the person who is ill or believes himself (or herslf) to be ill, seeks the advice of a doctor whom he (she) trusts. This is the consultation and all else in the practice of medicine derives from it
- **Objectives of the consultation:**
    - <u>Reason for presentation</u> - determine the exact reason for the presentation (note Masquerades)
    - <u>Management</u> - achive a good therapeutic outcome for the patient (different approaches with the same objective end-goal)
    - <u>Rapport</u> - develop a strong doctor-patient relationship

- **Consultation skills in primary care:**
    - Clinical skills
    - Diagnostic skills
    - Management skills
    - Communication skills
    - Education skills
    - Therapeutic skills
    - Manual skills
    - Counselling skills
- **The triphasic consultation** - 3 distinct phases:
    - <u>Rapport</u> - establishment of report and define the reason for presentation
    - <u>Diagnostic phase</u> - Hx, P/E, +/- Ix
    - <u>Management phase</u> - explanation and education, prescription, procedure, referral, follow-up
