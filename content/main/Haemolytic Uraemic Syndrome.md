# Haemolytic Uraemic Syndrome

- **Definition** - a thrombotic microangiopathy that preferentially involves small intra-renal vessels (uraemic), and is associated w/ micoangiopathic haemolytic anaemia (haemolytic)
- **Etiology of HUS** - 90% are diarrhoea-positive HUS:
    - **Diarrhoea-positive HUS (D+HUS)** - assicuated w/ GI infections in organisms that produce <u>verotoxins</u> (most commonly <u>enterohaemorrhagic E. coli</u> \[usually O157:H7 serotype\])
    - **Atypical HUS** (10%) - HUS w/ no infective cause and are classified into familial or sporadical HUS:
        - <u>Familial HUS</u> (penetrance incomplete) - due to mutations of genes encoding components of complement cascade, including factor H (CFH), factor B (CFB), and complement component 3 (C3)
        - <u>Sporadic HUS</u> - 70% of sporadic causes a/w development of autoAb to complement factor H
- **Clinical features of HUS** - most common cause of AKI in those w/ developed countries:
    - <u>Diarrhoeal disease</u> - classically a/w GI infections by enterohaemorrhagic E. coli in 90% of cases
    - <u>AKI</u> - pre-renal AKI, which may be severe and require RRT
    - <u>Bleeding</u> - as a result of severe thrombocytopenia
    - <u>Anaemia</u> - intravascular haemolysis anaemia often a/w jaundice
- **Ix:**
    - **Routine bloods** - CBC, LDH, haptoglobin, LRFT:
        - <u>CBC</u> - shows severe thrombocytopenia w/ macrocytic anaemia (and reticulocytosis)
        - <u>LDH</u> - markedly raised due to haemolysis
        - <u>Haptoglobin</u> - reduced
        - <u>LFT</u> - raised bilirubin (primarily unconjugated bilirubin)
        - <u>RFT</u> - AKI +/- electrolyte, acid-base abnormalities
    - **Peripheral blood smear** - shows schistocytes (fragmented blood smells), and polychromasia
- **Mx:**
    - <u>Supportive care</u> - e.g. fluid and electrolyte balance, transfusion and RRT if necessary
    - <u>FFP transfusion</u> - helpful in atypical HUS by replacement of complement components
    - <u>Plasma exchange</u> - removal of pathogenic autoantibody
    - <u>Eculizumab</u> - anti-C5 monoclonal Ab to prevent activation of terminal complement cascade
