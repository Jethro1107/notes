# Gastroparesis

- ****Definition**** - defective gastric emptying in the absence of mechanical obstruction of the stomach or duodenum
- ****Pathophysiology**** - primary or secondary defect of the gastric pacemaker:
    - ****Primary disorder**** - inherited or acquired disorder of the interstitial cells of Cajal
    - ****Secondary disorders:****
        - Disorders to autonomic nerve - e.g. autonomic neuropathy of DM
        - Disorders of musculature - e.g. systemic sclerosis, myotnoic dystrophies, amyloidosis
        - Anti-cholinergic S/E of drugs - Antidepressants, anticonvulsants, opiates, CCBs, GLP-1 agonists
        - Post-surgical - e.g. vagotomy, bariatric surgery, fundoplication
    - ****Trigger**** - post-viral infection, hyperglycaemia, ischaemia, electrolyte disturbances
- ****Clinical features of gastroparesisis:****
    - Vomiting - ?non-billous vomiting, and may contain food ingested several hours ago
    - Early satiety and abdominal fullness
    - Abdominal pain - rarely the predominant syndrome, but can be exacerbated by food
    - Nutritional failure - weight loss in severe case
- ****Signs of gastroparesis:****
    - Epigastric distension
    - Succession splash
    - Signs of underlying etiology:
        - Features of systemic sclerosis - Raynaud's phenomenon, scleroderma, fine crackles in lower lung fields
        - Features of autonomic dysfunction - orthostatic hypotension, pseudo-Argyll-Robertson pupil
- ****Diagnostic evaluation of gastroparesis:****
    - ****Principles of evaluation**** - demonstrate 1) absence of mechanical obstruction, 2) delayed gastric emptying, 3) precipitating cause:
        - Exclude GOO (esp for those w/ colicky abdominal pain) - Upper endoscopy, CT/MRI enterography
        - Gastric emptying study (diagnostic) - Scintigraphy for measuring gastric emptying
        - Ix for precipitating causes:
            - Routine bloods - CBC, RG, LRFT, CaPO4, TFT, blood gases
        - Pre-treatment evaluation - ECG (long-QT)
    - ****Gastric emptying study:****
        - Principles - Demonstrate excessive gastric retention (\>10% after 4h) using scintigraphy
        - Procedures:
            - Planning - control BG \< 15 mmol/L, withold drugs affecting gastric motility for 48-72h (opiates, antiematics, anticholinergics)
            - Imaging - standard imaging at 0, 1, 4h after 99mTc sulfer colloid labelled tagged meal
        - Interpretation - delayed gastric emptying if \> 10% retention at 4h
- ****Mx of gastroparesis:****
    - ****Principles of Mx:****
        - Lifestyle modifications - frequent, low fat meals
        - Pharmacological Mx - Prokinetic agents (e.g. metoclopramide, domperidone, erythromycin)
        - Surgical Mx - gastric electrical stimulation, gastric POEM
        - Nutritional support - long-term ejunostomy or long-term parenteral support
