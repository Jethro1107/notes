# Lactic Acidosis

- **Definition** - a form of HAGMA caused by an increase in lactate level (an organic acid)
- **Etiology of lactic acidosis:**
    - <u>Type 1 lactic acidosis</u> - due to anaerobic respiration as a result of tissue hypoxia and **peripheral generation of lactate**, commonly found in patients in **shock**
    - <u>Type 2 lactic acidosis</u> - due to impaired metabolism of lactate as in **liver disease**, drugs and toxins (**metformin**)
