# Visual Field Defect

- **Definition** - by definition complete loss of vision, or loss of part of the visual field, and is localised to any area between the retina and visual cortex
- **Approach to visual field loss** - ascertain pattern of visual field loss:
    - **Pattern of visual field loss** - see details below:
        - <u>Symptoms affecting one eye only</u> - localises to lesions **anterior to the optic chiasm** (retinal or optic disc)
        - <u>Symptoms affecting both eyes</u> - localises toe lesions **posterior to the optic chiasm, including the optic chiasm itself**
    - **Duration of visual field loss:**
        - <u>Sudden transient visual loss</u> (\< 15 min) - likely of vascular origin
        - <u>Persistent, or progressive visual loss</u> - points towards other etiologies
