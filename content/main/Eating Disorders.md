# Eating Disorders
- **Definition** - heterogenous groups of disorders characterised by abnormalities in pattern of eating,, the amount of food, and the nature of food being eaten
- **Distinct core psychopathology in ED:**
	- An overvaluation of weight and shape (judging self worth in terms of their shape and weight)
	- Concerning the need for control, i.e. the control of the shape and weight
- **Classification of eating disorders** - AN and BN reflect the best known, and most severe eating disorders, but many cases show significant features of either and do not meet the specific diagnostic criteria of either:
