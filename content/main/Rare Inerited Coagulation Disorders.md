# Rare Inherited Coagulation Disorders

- **Other rare deficiencies of coagulation factors** - all are AR disease:
    - Factor VII deficiency
    - Factor X deficiency
    - Factor XI deficiency
    - Factor XIII deficiency (not detectable in normal clotting profiles)
- **Clinical features of rare deficiencies of coagulation factors:**
    - Haemorrhage from umbilical stump
    - Intracranial haemorrhage
