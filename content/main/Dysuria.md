# Dysuria

\*- **Definition** - painful urination, often described as scalding, burning, or stinging, and often associated with suprapubic discomfort

- **DDx of dysuria:**
    - [[Lower Urinary Tract Infections and Cystitis]] - often accompanied by frequency, urgency, and feeling of incomplete emptying
    - STI - +ve sexual Hx, with urethral discharge
    - Other forms of bladder irritation - bladder stones, bladder carcinoma
