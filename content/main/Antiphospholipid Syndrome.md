# Antiphospholipid Syndrome

- Clinicopathological entity in which a constellation of clinical condition, alone or in combination, is found in association with persistently positive test for an anti-phospholipid antibodies.
    - May occur in isolation, or secondary to an associated conditions
- Clinical conditions a/w APS (secondary APS)
    - SLE (prototypical)
    - Other rheumatological conditions - RA, SSc, Sjogren's syndrome, Giant cell arteritis, Behcet's syndrome
- Types of anti-phospholipid antibodies
    - Anti-cardiolipin Ab (IgG, IgM)
    - Anti-beta-2 glycoprotein I
    - Lupus anticoagulant (double misnomer, not typically related to lupus, and isn't an anticoagulant in the body)
- Pathophysiology of APS (not fully known)
    - Targets Protein C - thrombotic tendancies
    - Targets Prothrombin - bleeding tendancies
    - Other targets - beta-2-glycoprotein 1, Annexin V
- Clinical manifestations of APS
    - Adverse pregnancy outcomes - recurrent 1st trimester fetal loss (\>= 3), unexplained death of morphologically normal fetus after 10wks of gestation, severe early pre-eclampsia
    - Arterial and venous thrombosis
    - Other manifestations - livedo reticularis, catastrophic APS, transverse myelitis, skin necrosis, chorea
- Laboratory findings in APS
    - Persistently +ve Anticardiolipin Ab test - antibodies binding to -ve charged phospholipids on ELISA plate
    - Interference with APTT test with abnormal 1:1 mixing test
- Dx criteria of APS - Dx extremely important as it alters Mx of arterial thrombosis, VTE and pregnancy
    - Laboratory criteria - Persistent positive Ab (12 weeks apart)
    - Clinical criteria - clinically manifests as arterial thrombus or venous thrombus, or pregnancy complications
- Tx of APLS
    - Treatment of VTE - LMWH + bridge to warfarin in patients (DOACs not effective)
    - Treatment of Arterial thrombus - ?add aspirin on top of warfarin? (Davidson + slides)
    - Pregnancy prophylaxis - heparin + aspirin
