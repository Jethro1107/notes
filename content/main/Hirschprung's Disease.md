# Hirschprung's Disease

- **Definition** - congenital absence of ganglion cells in the large intestine, characterised by constipation and megacolon
- **Epidemiology:**
    - _Incidence_ - 1:5000 births
    - _Demographic_ - male preponderance (M:F = 8:1)
- **Genetics of Hirschprung's disease:**
    - Only 1/3 of patients have +ve FHx, where typically inherited in an AD pattern w/ incomplete penetrance
    - LOF mutation of _RET proto-oncogene_ implicated in 50% of familial cases and 15% of sporadic cases
    - However, some patients w/ LOF RET mutation do not develop clinical disease, possibly due to necessary gene-gene interaction
- **Pathophysiology:**
    - _Abnormal enteric neurogenesis_ - all implicated genes of Hirschprung's disease are involved in regulation of enteric neurogenesis, w/ mutation causing **failure of migration of neuroblasts into gut wall during embryogenesis**
    - _Internal sphincteric dysfunction_ - absence of ganglion cells in the enteric plexus usually in a short segment of the rectum +/- sigmoid colon, resulting in **failed relaxation of the internal anal sphincter**
- **Clinical features of Hirschprung's disease** - Sx since onset, but few cases do not present until childhood or adolescence:
    - Constipation
    - Marked abdominal distension (congenital megacolon)
    - Vomiting
- **PR examination** - empty rectum
- **Ix:**
    - **Barium enema** - narrowed rectum w/ colonic dilatation proximal to narrowed segments
    - **Anal manometry** - failure of rectum to relax w/ balloon distension
    - **Full thickness Bx:**
    - _H&E staining_ - absence of ganglion cells
    - _Histochemical staining_ - -ve for acetylcholinesterase
- **Mx** - resection of affected segments
