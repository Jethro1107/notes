# Environmetal and Genetic Factors of Cancer

- **Cancer as a result of interaction between genetic factor and exposure to environmental hormones** - hence the majority of cancers do not have a single attributable cause
- **Environmental factors** - association is often weak, with evidence of a multifactorial pathogenesis even when their is a principal environmental cause (i.e. not all smokers develop bronchial carcinoma): 
![](img/environmental_factors_that_predispose_to_cancer.png)
- **Genetic factors** - inherited cancer predisposition syndromes account for 5-10% of all cancers, but none have 100% penetrance:
