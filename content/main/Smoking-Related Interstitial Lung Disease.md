# Smoking-Related Interstitial Lung Disease

- **Definition and terminology** - various ILDs where smoking is thought to be a result of active or prior tobacco smoke exposure:
    - **Disease entities subclassified into IIPs:**
        - <u>Respiratory bronchiolitis with interstitial lung disease</u> (RB-ILD)
        - <u>Desquamative interstitial pneumonia</u> (DIP)
    - **Disease entities not under IIPs:**
        - <u>Pulmonary Langerhan's cell histocytosis</u> (PLCH)
- **Epidemiology** - felt to account for \< 10% of IIPs
- **HRCT findings in smoking-related ILDs:**
    - <u>Characteristic findings in RB-ILD</u>:
        - Central and peripheral bronchial wall thickening
        - Centrilobular nodules
        - Ground glass opacities
        - Septal lines and reticular pattern not uncommon
    - <u>Characteristic findings in DIP</u> - usually diffuse (ocassionally patchy) bilateral symmetrical GGOs more predominant
- **Histopathology of smoking-related ILDs:**
    - Chronic inflammation of respiratory bronchiolar walls, w/ pigmented macrophage infiltrates, and both bronchiolar and peribronchiolar fibrosis resulting in architectural distortion
    - Changes are patchy in RB-ILDs, but more diffuse in DIPs
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Smoking cessation</u> - counselled to discontinue smoking or enroll in formal smoking cessation programe
        - <u>Pharmacological therapy</u> - immunosuppressives, cytotixcs, and ocassional use of bronchodilators w/ no strong evidence
