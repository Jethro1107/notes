# Lymphoma of the Small Bowel

- **Definition** - lymphoma (NHL) involvement of the gastrointestinal tract as part of a more generalised disease or arising de novo in the gut, most commonly the small bowel
- **Associated conditions** - increasing gut involvement w/:
    - <u>Enteropathy</u> - most a/w coeliac disease (enteropathy-associated T-cell lymphoma)
    - <u>Immunodeficiency states</u> - most commonly HIV-AIDs (B cell origin), although other immunocomprimised states may be implicated
- **Clinical manifestations of SB lymphoma** - constitutional 'B' Sx marked if high-grade lymphoma:
    - <u>Small bowel obstruction</u> - may be complete or incomplete, usually a/w colicky abdominal pain, other features of obstruction like N/V, and rarely perforation
    - <u>Weight loss</u> - attributed to various mechanisms, 1) constitutional Sx, 2) malabsorptive states
    - <u>Malabsorption</u> - sign of **diffuse GI disease**; diarrhoea and weight loss, a/w various effects of macro- and micro-nutrient deficiency
    - <u>Protein-losing enteropathy</u> - presenting w/ unexplained hypoalbuminaemia and frank anarsarca
- **Signs of SB lymphoma:**
    - <u>Lymphadenopathy</u> - may be present if as part of a more generalised disease
    - <u>Hepatosplenomegaly</u> - very rare
- **Ix and Dx:**
    - <u>Contrast CT</u> - identification of mass and evaluation of disease extent
    - <u>Enteroscopy</u> - small bowel biopsy
    - <u>Additional Ix</u>:
        - Staging Ix (e.g. PETCT)
        - Pre-Tx evaluation as in other haematological malignancies
- **Mx:**
    - <u>Definitive Tx</u> - surgical resection of isolated SB disease
    - <u>Mx of advanced disease</u> - isolated chemotherapy/ chemoRT
