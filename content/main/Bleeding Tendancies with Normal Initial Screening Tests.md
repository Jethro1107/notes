# Bleeding Tendancies with Normal Initial Screening Tests

- **Definition** - clinically suspected bleeding disorder despite having normal PLT, PT and aPTT
- **DDx of patients w/ bleeding tendancy but normal initial screening** - up to 30-60% of patients sent to tertiary centres will have a negative evaluation:
    - <u>Von Willebrand disease</u> - inherited von Willebrand disease (most common) or acquired von-Willebrand syndrome
    - <u>Platelet function disorders</u> - acquired (esp. drug use), or congenital
    - <u>Disorders of fibrin cross-link formation and fibrinolysis</u>:
        - Inherited factor XIII deficiency
        - Disorders of fibrinolysis - congenital (e.g. deficiency of anti-fibrinolytic agents) or acquired (e.g. hyperfibrinosis in APML, DIC or prostate cancer)
    - <u>Vascular or connective tissue disorders</u> - e.g. HHT, Scurvy, Vascular Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, osteogenesis imperfecta
- **Ix:**
    - **VWD testing** - including plasma VWF antigen, plasma VWF activity, plasma factor VIII activity, analysis of multimers:
        - **Components of WVD testing:**
            - <u>Plasma VWF Ag</u> (VWF:Ag)
            - <u>Plasma VWF activity</u> (VWF:RCo, Gp1b binding activity, or VWF:CB) - assess binding actiivity of vWF to collagen or glycoprotein
            - <u>Plasma factor VII activity</u> - impaired protection of factor VIII results in reduced levels of factor VIII despite normal aPTT
            - <u>Analysis of monomers</u> - indicated if VWF:RCo to VWF:Ag ratio \< 0.7, i.e. suggestive of a dysfunctional VWF

        - **Interpretations** - level of VWF dependent on blood group, estrogen, stress, exercise, inflammation or ongoing bleeding, hence repeated assays may be required
    - **Platelet function testing** - usually no drug-induced or acquired causes identified:
        - <u>Platelet aggregation studies</u> - platelet aggregation studies measure platelet aggregation induced by known agonists (e.g. ADP, collagen, thrombin, epinephrine), useful in distinguishing congenital disorders
        - <u>PFA-100</u> - platelet function analyser measures time taken for blood flow to stop under shear stress in tube containing agonists for haemostasis
        - <u>Genetic testing</u> - for known platelt function disorders
        - <u>Electron microscopy</u> - visualisation of ultrastructure of platelet granules in suspected platelet storage pool diseases
    - **Ix for disorders of fibrin cross link and fibrinolysis:**
        - Factor XIII assay
        - D dimer and fibrinogen assay for hyperfibrinolysis
