# Chapter 1: Obstetric History Taking and Examination

## History

- **Components of obstetric Hx:**
    - <u>Dating the pregnancy</u> - historically dated from the first day of LMP, not the day of conception
    - <u>Social Hx</u> - implications of social support (e.g. relationship with father) on Mx, economic stability, smoking, alcohol, and illicit drug intake
    - <u>Previous obstetric Hx</u> - list the pregnancies in date and discover outcomes of each pregnancy in order to establish risk in the current pregnancy
    - <u>Past gynaecological Hx</u> - regularity of menses, past contraceptive use, important in dating, cervical smear Hx, ectopic pregnancies, recurrent miscarriage
    - <u>Past medical and surgical Hx</u> -
    - <u>Drug Hx</u> - drugs that may influence the pregnancy
    - <u>Family Hx</u> - Hx of medical, psyciatric disorders that may have implications for the fetus and baby
