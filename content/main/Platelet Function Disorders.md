# Platelet Function Disorders

- **Definition** - heterogenous groups of disorders characterised by loss of platelet function, which may be congenital or acquired
- **Types of platelet function disorder:**
    - **Congenital disorders:**
        - <u>Glanzmann's thromboasthenia</u> - inherited disorder a/w reduced Gp IIb/IIIa function during platelet aggregation
        - <u>Bernard-Soulier disease</u> - inherited disorder a/w reduced Gp Ib function
        - <u>Storage pool disorders</u> - deficiency of dense granules within platelets
        - <u>Congenital macrothrombocytopathies</u> - mutations in myosin heavy chain a/w alterations of platelet function and other clinical features
    - **Acquired disorders:**
        - <u>Iatrogenic/ Drug-induced</u> (most common) - aspirin, clopidogrel, IIb/IIIa inhibitors, dipyradamole
        - <u>Medical comorbidities</u> - e.g. uraemia
- **Clinical features** - typically manifests as bleeding tendancy after trauma (rarely spontaneous except glanzmann's thrombasthenia)
- **Mx** - dependent on underlying condition:
    - <u>Local meassures</u> - e.g. prevention of excessive bleeding by mechanical means
    - <u>Tranexemic acid</u> - may be useful in severe bleeding
    - <u>Recombinant VIIa</u> - licensed for Tx of resistant bleeding only in Glanxzmann's thromboasthenia
