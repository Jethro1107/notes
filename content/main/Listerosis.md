# Listerosis

- **Definition** - an environmental gram-positive bacilus which can contaminate food sources, and is a medically important cause of bacterial gastroenteritis, bacteraemia, and bacterial meningitis
- **Microbiology of Listeria monocytogenes:**
    - **Gram stain and microscopy** - aerobic G+ rods
    - **Food-bourne transmission** - known to cause outbreaks of gastroenteritis in immunocompetent individuals:
        - <u>Cold-enrichment</u> - characteristically outgrows other contaminating bacteria during refrigeration
        - <u>Commonly contaminated foods</u> - raw vegetables, soft cheese, undercooked chicken, fish, meat and pates
    - **ABx resistance:**
        - Inherently resistance against cephalosporin
        - Sensitive to aminopenicillin (e.g. ampicillin) +/- aminoglycosides
- **Clinical manifestations of listerosis** - non-specific Sx of fever and myalgia invariably present:
    - <u>Bacterial gastroenteritis</u> - have been reported in immunocompetent host through ingestion of contaminated food
    - <u>Systemic invasive infections</u> - in susceptible individuals:
        - Chorioamnionitis - in pregnancies resulting in fetal deaths, abortpions and neonatal infections
        - Bacterial meningitis - presents w/ fever, confusion, meningism, however CSF may show neutrophilic pleocytosis w/ **normal glucose**
        - Bacteraemia - fever w/o a foci of infection
- **Risk factors for systemic invasive listerosis:**
    - Pregnancy
    - Elderly (\> 55y)
    - Immunocomprimised
- **Ix** - readily culturable by blood or CSF cultures
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Bacterial meningitis</u> - add on ampicilin to empirical ABx therapy for meningitis (i.e. cephalosporin, which is not useful) for those at risk of listerosis
        - <u>Most effective ABx regimen</u>:
            - IV aminopenicillin (amoxicillin or ampicillin) + aminoglycoside
            - Co-trimoxazole + aminoglycoside in those w/ penicillin allergy
- **Prevention:**
    - <u>Proper Tx of food</u> - do not undercook food
    - <u>Avoid high-risk products</u> - avoid soft cheeses during pregnancy
