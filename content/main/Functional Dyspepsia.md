# Functional Dyspepsia

- **Definition of functional dyspepsia** - chronic dyspepsia in the absence of organic disease
- **Clinical features:**
    - **Varying symptoms** - Epigastric pain, early satiety, post-prandial fullness, bloating and nausea.
    - **Two clinical entities w/ overlap:**
        - **Post-prandial distress syndrome** (dysmotility-like) - nausea, satiety, and bloating a/w meals
        - **Epigastric pain syndrome** (ulcer like) - epigastric pain or burning, not necessarily related to meals or defecation
- **Dx of functional dyspepsia:**
    - **Pre-requisite** - r/o of organic cause after Hp testing and upper endoscopy (diagnosis by exclusion)
    - **Rome IV criteria** - Presence of \>= 1 of the following that cannot be explained by any organic cause:
        - Bothersome postprandial fullness (3 d/w)
        - Bothersome early satiety (3 d/w)
        - Bothersome epigastric pain (1 d/w)
        - Bothersome epigastric burning (1 d/w)
- **Mx of functional dyspepsia:**
    - Reassurance - explain that no organic pathologies
    - Lifestyle modifications - dietary changes, low fat diet
    - Hp eradication - 10% patient experience symptomatic relieve
    - Continue empirical drug Tx:
        - PPI +/- TCA and prokinetics (metoclopramide for refractory cases)
    - Psychotherapy
