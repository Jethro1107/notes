# Drug-induced Thyroiditis

- **Drugs associated w/ thyroiditis:**
    - Amiodarone
    - INF-alpha
    - Immune-checkpoint inhibitors (routine monitoring of TFT recommended)
    - Tyrosine kinase inhibitors
    - Lithium
