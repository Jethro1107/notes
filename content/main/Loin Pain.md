# Loin Pain

- **Etiology of loin pain:**
    - Musculoskeltal disease (most common)
    - Renal stone disease - often radiates anteriorly and to groin, and characteristically with microscopic haematuri
    - Acute pyelonephritis
    - Renal tumours
    - Other urinary tract obstructions - e.g. congenital abnormalities of PUJ
