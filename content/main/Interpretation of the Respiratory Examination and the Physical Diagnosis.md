# Interpretation of the Respiratory Examination and the Physical Diagnosis

- **Rationale:**
    - Interpretation of the chest examination taken as a whole
    - Constellation of signs (i.e. Sign sets) implies a "physical diagnosis", which is a pathological process (cf clinical diagnosis which is a disease entity)
    - Reporting of sign sets should follow a predictive pattern, rather than being random and haphazard
    - The "physical diagnosis" should be able to predict the radiographical features if performed
    - Each "physical diagnosis" will entail a list of differential diagnoses
- **5 "physical diagnoses" to know at MBBS level:**
    - Consolidation
    - Collapse
    - Pleural effusion
    - Pneumothorax
    - Fibrosis
