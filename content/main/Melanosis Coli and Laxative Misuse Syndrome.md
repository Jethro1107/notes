# Melanosis Coli and Laxative Misuse Syndrome

- **Definition and terminology:**
    - _Melanosis coli_ - brown discoloration of the colonic mucosa due to long-term consumption of stimulant laxatives leading to accumulation of lipofuscin pigments in local macrophages
    - _Laxative misuse syndrome_ - megacolon or 'cathartic colon' characterised by lost of haustra, and shortening of bowel resulting in refractory diarrhoea
- **Clinical association:**
    - Bulinimia nervosa
    - Anorexia nervosa
- **Clinical presentation:**
    - _Profuse watery diarrhoea_ - typically complaint of refractory water diarrhoea while denying laxative use
- **Ix:**
    - **AXR** - acquired megacolon (cathartic colon)
    - **Endoscopy:**
    - _Melanosis coli_ - brown pigmentation resembling 'tiger skin' caused by accumulation of lipofuscin pigments in the lamina propria
    - _Featureless mucosa_ - lost of haustra, shortening of bowel
