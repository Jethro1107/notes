# Meniscal Tear

- **Mechanism of injury:**
	- Compression of the meniscus w/ knee in semi-flexed abduction (medial meniscus) and lateral meniscus
	- Compounded by twisting motion on a weight-loaded flexed knee
	- ![[Pasted image 20260522175007.png]]
- **Clinical suspicion of meniscal injuries:**
	- Hx of twisting motion on a weight-bearing knee
	- Unexplained onset of knee pain a/w locking/ catching Sx
- **Onset of meniscal injuries w/ minor trauma** - presentation in 30-50y due to meniscal degeneration:
	- Cleavage tears of posterior horn of medial meniscus
	- Parrot beak tears of mid-section of lateral meniscus
- **Clinical features of meniscal tear:**
	- ![[Pasted image 20260522175427.png]]
- **Ix:**
	- _X-ray of knee_ - special views required to review loose bodies but are non-specific
	- _MRI knee_ - typically required to confirm the diagnosis
- **Mx:**
	- Trial of physiotherapy
	- Arthroscopic meniscectomy
