# Benign Laryngeal Conditions

- **Vocal cord nodules:**
    - **Definition** - benign nodules on the vocal cords classically caused by voice abuse
    - **Classical occupational association:**
        - Singers
        - Teachers
    - **Pathophysiology** - repeated vocal trauma:
        - Localised oedema
        - Fibrosis and nodule formation
    - **Clinical features:**
        - <u>Dysphonia</u> - persistent husky voice
        - <u>Easy fatigue on vocalisation</u> - easy fatigue as vocal cords unable to oppose resulting in air leak
    - **Signs** - always <u>bilateral and symmetrical</u> nodules at the <u>junction of anterior- and middle-third of the vocal folds</u>: 
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/vocal_cord_nodules_on_laryngoscopy.png)
    - **Mx:**
        - Speech therapy
        - Vocal hygiene
    - Pathophysiology:
        - Vocal trauma (forceful closure of cord durng forceful phonation) –\> localised oedema –\> fibrosis –\> nodules
        - Characteristically occurs in junction between anterior and middle 1/3 of vocal folds bilaterally and symmetrically (because vibrates the most)
    - Clinical features - husky voice and easy fatigue
    - Mx:
        - Speech therapy
        - Vocal hygiene
- **Vocal polyp:**
    - **Definition** - unilateral vocal cord lesion associated w/ vocal trauma
    - **Pathophysiology** - acute vocal trauma:
        - Bleeding from mucosal vessels organised into a haematoma
        - Haematoma transforms to polyp
    - **Clinical features:**
        - <u>Dysphonia</u> - persistent husky voice
        - <u>Easy fatigue on vocalisation</u> - easy fatigue as vocal cords unable to oppose resulting in air leak
    - **Signs** - unilateral vocal cord polyp: 
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/vocal_cord_polyp.png)
    - **Mx** - microlaryngoscopy + excision
- **Reinke's edema:**
    - **Definition** - fluid collection (or myxoedema) in the Reinke's space, in the superficial lamina propria of the vocal cord
    - **Etiology** - associated with certain medical conditions:
        - Smoking
        - GERD
        - Hypothyroidism (myxoedema deposition)
    - **Clinical features:**
        - <u>Dysphonia</u> - often **effortful**, **low-pitch and rough** voice
    - **Signs:** 
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/reinkes_oedema.png)
    - **Mx:**
        - Correct underlying cause
        - Microlaryngoscopy + Excision
- **Recurrent respiratory papillomatosis:**
    - **Definition** - recurrent papilloma formation on vocal cord caused by chronic HPV infection (subtypes 6 and 11)
    - **Aquisition of HPV infection:**
        - In utero transfer
        - Birth tract contact
        - Oral tract
    - **Clinical features:**
        - <u>Dyspnonia</u> - recurrent hoarseness
        - <u>Upper airway obstruction</u> - manifests as stridor/ SOB before complete obstruction
        - <u>Malignant transformation</u> - risk of malignant transformation to SCC
    - **Signs:** 
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/benign_respiratory_papillomatosis.png)
    - **Mx:**
        - Microlaryngoscopy + excision
        - HPV vaccine reduces recurrence
        - Adjuvant medical therapy (e.g. avastin, cidovovir, interferon-alpha)
