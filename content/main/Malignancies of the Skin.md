# Malignancies of the Skin

- **Classification of skin malignancies:**
	- **Melanoma** - malignancy from epidermal melanocytes
	- **Non-melanoma skin cancer** (NMSC):
		- _Basal cell carcinoma_ - the most common skin cancer
		- _Squamous cell carcinoma_ - invasive neoplasm defined as malignancy arising from squamous cells of epidermis passing through the basement membrane, w/ pre-invasive states (intra-epithelial carcinoma; Bowen's disease; BD) and dysplastic states (actinic keratosis; AK)
- **Role of UVR in pathogenesis of skin malignancy** - complete carcinogen and is the main environmental risk factor for skin cancer:
	- _Epidemiological data_:
		- Skin cancer is much more comon in countries w/ high ambient sun exposure, such as Australia
		- Overall skin cancer risk also increased if imigrated to a country w/ high sun exposure, particularly at a younger age, which supports an environmental factor rather than being a genetic factor
		- SCC and AK, and to a lesser extent BCC, is a/w chronic UVR exposure
		- Melanoma usually arises from bursts of intermittent UVR exposure, and episodes of sunburn have been implicated as risk factors of melanoma

- **Genetic predispositions of skin cancer:**
	- _Xeroderma pigmentosum_ - autosomal recessive condition caused by inherit defect in DNA excision repair
	- _Basal cell naevus (Gorlin's) syndrome_ - autosomal dominant disorder in which PTCH tumour suppressor gene on Chromosome 9 is defective
- **Immunosuppression** - loss of cutaneous immune surveillance:
	- _Anti-rejection medications_ - organ transplant recipients are a/w high risk of skin malignancies in the long run
	- _Phototherapy_ - high numbers of PUVA phototherapy a/w  increased risk of SCC, but surprisingly not UVB
- **Chronic dermatological conditions** - chronic inflammation as a risk factor for SCC:
	- Chronic skin ulcers (e.g. marjolin's ulcer)
	- Chronic skin inflammation
	- Dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa
- **Other environmental exposures:**
	- _Other forms of radiation_ - thermal radiation, ionising radiation (radiotherapy), and PUVA treatments (particular SCC)
	- _Chemical carcinogens_ - exposure to arsenic or coal tar increases NMSC risk
	- _Hyman papillomavirus_ - role of oncogenic HPV particularly in immunocomprimised hosts, where > 80% of those developing NMSC harbor the virus
