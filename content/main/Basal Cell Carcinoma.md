# Basal Cell Carcinoma

- **Definition** - a form of NMSC arising from immature pluripotent epidermal cells found in the basal layer of the epidermis
- **Epidemiology** - most common form of skin cancer (70% of NMSC):
	- _Incidence_ - increasing dramatically over the past 30y
	- _Demographic_:
		- Age - increasing incidence w/ age
		- Sex - male preponderance
- **Mood of spread of BCC** - often thought as a malignant tumour that rarely metastasises
	- _Local invasion_ - local invasion in the skin, often a/w ulceratioin (hence termed 'rodent ulcer')
	- _Lymphatic and haematogenous spread_ - rarely distant metastasis
- **Molecular pathogenesis of BCC** - PTCH tumour suppressor gene (cf Gorlin's syndrome) identified a/w aberrant hedgehog signalling pathway
- **Clinical features of BCC** - typically on sites of moderate sun exposure (esp. face), and are slow growing:
	- **Nodular BCC** - often early BCCs presenting as _pale, translucent nodules or papules_, w/ overlying _superficial telangiectactic vessels_
	- **Superficial BCC** - _red, brown plaque_ or patch w/ _raised, thread-like edge_
	- **Superficial multifocal BCC** - composed of multiple nodular lesions, often may be large (up to 10cm) and occurs on the trunk
	- **Morphoeic, infiltrative BCC** - ill-defined, slowly enlarging, sclerotic yellow/ grey plaque
- **Mx:**
	- **Principles of Mx** - mode of Rx dependent on patient factors and lesion factors:
		- _Patient factors_ - e.g. patient age, general frailty, association w/ Gorlin's syndrome
		- _Lesion factors_ - risk of lesion, type of lesion, site of lesion
	- **Modalities of Tx:**
		- _Surgical Tx_ - Moh's micrographic surgery aiming at complete resection and margin controls most effective for superficial BCCs and is a/w lowest recurrence rates
		- _Phototherapy_ - topical photodynamic phototheray w/ topical imiquimod effective for superficial BCCs
		- _Other physical treatments_ - e.g. cryotherapy and curettage and cautoaury for selected low risk Tx
		- _Radiotherapy_ - may be used for large lesions in frail patients
