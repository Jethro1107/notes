# Infectious Mononucleosis and Ebstein-Barr Virus

- **Definition** - a clinical syndrome characterised by fever, pharyngitis, and cervical lymphadenopathy, most often caused by the Epstein-Barr virus
- **Etiology of infectious mononucleosis-like syndrome:**
    - EBV
    - CMV
    - HHV-6/7
    - Primary HIV infection
    - Toxoplasmosis
- **Epstein-Barr virus** - gamma herpesvirus often w/ subclinical infections in early childhood or early adolesence:
    - <u>Transmission</u> - saliva, droplet transmission, fomites
    - <u>Risk of clinically significant infections</u> - age dependent:
        - Subclinical in childhood
        - 50% IM-like syndrome in young adulthood
        - More severe and prolonged clinical manifestations
- **Clinical features of IM** - prolonged and indetermined incubation period w/ a prodromal period:
    - **Prodrome** - characterised by non-specific Sx such as fever, headache and malaise
    - **Classical triad of infectious mononucleosis** - fever, severe pharyngitis, and lymphadenopathy:
        - <u>Fever</u> - high fever but generally resolves within 2 weeks
        - <u>Pharyngitis</u> - often severe w/ tonsilar exudates (rarely causing airway obstruction) and palatal petechiae
        - <u>Lymphadenopathy</u> - non tender anterior and posterior lymphadenopathy often a/w:
            - Periorbital oedema
            - Inguinal and axillary oedema
            - Macular, petichial or erythema multiforme rash
- **Complications of infectious mononucleosis:**
    - **Non-malignant conditions:**
        - <u>Complications of the upper airway</u> - severe pharyngeal oedema, respiratory obstruction
        - <u>Complications w/ neurological involvement</u> - polyneuritis, transverse myelitis, encephalitis (CN palsies)
        - <u>Complications w/ haematological involvement</u> - haemolytic anaemia, thrombocytopenia
    - **EBV-associated malignancies:**
        - <u>Immunocompetent individuals</u> - nasopharyngeal carcinoma, HL, Burkitt's lymphoma
        - <u>Immunocomprimised individuals</u> - primary CNS lymphoma, post-transplant lymphoproliferative disease

- **Ix:**
    - <u>PBS</u> - atypical lymphocytes, although common in EBV infections but can also occur in other causes of IM, acute retroviral syndrome, viral hepatitis, mumps and rubella
    - <u>Monospot test</u> - detection of heterophile Ab
    - <u>EBV serology</u> - +ve results w/ IgM Ab (sero-conversion of anti-EBNA approximately 1mo after initial illness)
    - <u>Throat swab</u> - for bacterial pharyngitis
    - <u>LP</u> - if suspecting CNS infection
- **DDx of atypical lymphocytes on PBS:**
    - IM-like syndrome
    - Viral hepatitis
    - Acute retroviral syndrome of HIV infection
    - Mumps
    - Rubella
    - Lymphoproliferative disorders
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Supportive care</u> - antipyrexics, bed rest, +/- corticosteroids for severe pharyngeal oedema
        - <u>ABx</u> - witheld and only penicillin if throat culture yields GAS (amoxicillin/ ampicillin causes rash)
        - <u>Physical activity</u> - witheld until splenomegaly resolves due to risk of splenic rupture
