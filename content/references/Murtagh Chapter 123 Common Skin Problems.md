## Tinea

- **Definition** - also known as ringworm infections, a fungal infection caused by 3 major classes of dermatophytic organisms that have the ability to invade and proliferate in keratin of the skin, nails, and hair
- **Dx:**
	- _Skin scraping and microscopy_ (10% KOH) - demonstration of encrouching septate hyphae
	- _Fungal culture_ - considered gold standard but seldom performed
- **Tinea pedis** (athlete's foot):
	- **Definition** - dermatophytosis of the foot, typically between the digits
	- **Epidemiology** - most common fungal infections in human
	- **Etiological agent** - trichophyton rubrum
	- **Clinical features** - typically affects in between the 4th-5th toes, and also between the 3rd-4th toes:
		- Pruritis
		- Foot odour
		- Scaling, masseration, and fissuring of the affected skin
	- **Mx:**
		- **General advice:**
			- _Keep feet clean and dry_ - thoroughly dry feet after bathing and showering; wear sandals in public showers rather than barefeet
			- _Maintain hygiene_ - use of antifungal powder especially in between toes after drying, removal of flaky skin using dry tissue paper; change shoes and socks daily
			- _Proper aeration_ - go barefoot if possible; prefer sandals over shoes; wear light cotton/ wool socks to maintain aeration
		- **Pharmacological Tx:**
			- _Topical antifungals_ - prefer terbinafine 1% od for 1 week and review, other options (bid or tid for 2-3 weeks) include:
				- Clotrimazole 1%
				- Ketoconazole 2%
				- Terbinafine 1% cream or gel
				- Miconazole 2% cream or lotion
				- Bifonazole 1%
			- _Oral antifungals_ - griseofulvin or terbinafine for up to 6 weeks
- **Tinea corporis:**
	- **Definition** - ringworm infection of the body
	- **Etiological agents:**
		- Trichophyton rubrum (60%)
		- Microsporum canis
	- **Associated exposures:**
		- Cats and dogs
		- Guinea pigs (facial tinea)
	- **Clinical features of tinea corporis** - often characterised by _pruritic_, _erythematous_ _spreading lesions_:
		- Spreading circular erythematous lesions, w/ _slight scaling or vesicles at the edge_, but _relatively normal centre_
		- May involve the hair, feet, and nails

			- Clotrimazole 1%
			- Ketoconazole 2%

- **Tinea capitis:**
	- **Definition** - ringworm infection of the scalp
	- **Etiological agents** - Microsporum canis
