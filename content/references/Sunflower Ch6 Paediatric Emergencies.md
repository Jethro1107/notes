# Ch6 Paediatric Emergencies

## The seriously ill child

- **Clinical presentation of a seriously ill child** - impending cardiorespiratory arrest:
    - Fitting child
    - Comatosed child
    - Child with shock
    - Child with respiratory distress
    - Child w/ surgical emergency (e.g. surgical abdomen)
- **Common etiology depending on clinical presentation of a seriously ill child:** 
![](img/clinical_presentation_and_causes_of_seriously_ill_child.png)
- **Normal vital signs differ for age:**
    - **Respiratory rate:**
        - <u>Infant</u> - 30-40/min
        - <u>Young children</u> - 25-30/min
        - <u>Older children</u> - 20-25/min
    - **Heart rate:**
        - <u>Infant</u> - 110-160 bpm
        - <u>Young children</u> - 95-140 bpm
        - <u>Older children</u> - 80-120 bpm
    - **SBP:**
        - <u>Infant</u> - 70-90 mmHg
        - <u>Young children</u> - 80-100 bpm
        - <u>Older children</u> - 90-110 bpm

  
  ![](img/normal_vitals_in_paediatrics.png)
- **Approach to to seriously ill child:**
    - <u>Primary accessment</u> - Airway and C-spine, Breathing, Circulation, Disability and Exposure
    - <u>Resuscitaiton</u> - establish monitoring, IV access, and maneuvers for ABC
    - <u>Secondary assessment</u> - targeted Hx from informant, targeted P/E, Ix
    - <u>Intervention</u> - other Ix and Mx

  
  ![](img/assessment_of_seriously_ill_child.png)
