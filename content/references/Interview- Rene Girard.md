# Notes

- **Girard on post-structuralist's writing:** Reflects his view on the validity of critical writing is based on intellectual clarity and economy of writing. The validity of a concept stems from it's reductive power, to reduce a complex phenomena into their essential dynamics, rather than shrouding them with their overly convoluted language. The rigor of thought is replaced by a performative prose in the current landscape.

  The essence of intellectual abstraction of concepts lies in making them more economical, accessible without sacrificing depth… Simply put, Girard believes that in two competing hypothesis for the same phenomenon, he will always choose the more elegant option, the one that <u>uses less to explain more</u>. While it is true that the reality may be more complex, fluid, ambiguous, the aim is to translate this complexity into impenetrable writing. The goal is to clarify, not mystify. The Girardian theory on the human psyche, on community, and culture rests on the single irrefutable fact that <u>humans are have the tendancy and capacity for mimesis</u>, and that easily flows into a kalidoscope of implications. This reductionist approach is what allowed Girard to trace complex social and psychological phenomena back to a single human dynamic, providing a coherent, and far-reaching explanatory framework.
