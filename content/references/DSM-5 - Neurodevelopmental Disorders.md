## Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder
- **Defining ADHD:**
	- Persistent pattern of inattention and/or hyperactivity/ impulsivity
	- Symptoms were present prior to age 12y
	- Several inattention or hyperactive symptoms occurs in two or more settings
	- Interferring with social, acaedemic, or occupational functioning
	- Not occurring exclusively in the course of a psychotic disorder or schizophrenia, and not better explained by another episode
- **Inattention:**
	- _Sustaining attention in tasks_
	- _Attention to details_
	- _Sustaining attention in conversation_
	- _Preference in tasks requiring sustained 
	- _Instruction following_
	- _Organising tasks and activities_
	- _Distractibility_
	- _Losing necessary items_
	- _Forgetfulness_
- **Hyperactivity:**
	- **Uncomfortable with stillness:**
		- _Fidgetting and squirming_
		- _Leaving seats_
		- _On the go or driven by motor_
	- **Noise:**
		- _Talking excessively_
		- _Loudness_
- **Impulsivity:**
	- _Blurting out answers_
	- _Waiting turns_
	- _Interrupting or intruding on otheres_
