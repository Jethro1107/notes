# Ch1 Classification of Psychiatric Disorder

- **Principles of classification:**
    - The ideal classification system should be based on etiology, however the exact etiology of most psychiatric disorders are not known
    - The current pragmatic approach to classification is through defining syndromes which may or may not be true disease entities, i.e. a constellation of S/S that are unique as a group:
        - Some clinical syndromes were subsequently shown to be true disease entities as they essentially one cause (or one dominant cause)
        - Some clinical syndromes remain as syndromes and are shown to have different etiologies
- **Syndromes and diseases:**
    - Syndromes - constellation of S/S that are unique as a group, where it is the particular combination of Sx that makes the syndrome specific
