# Part 2: Chain of Survival

- **Definition of chain of survival** - emergency cardiovascular care systems-of-care concepts describing actions that must take place to give the cardiac arrest victim the best chance of survival, which may differ with the context of arrest
- **Action in chain of survival** - differ according to setting (in-hospital vs out-of-hospital) and age group (paediatric vs adult): 
![](img/chain_of_survival_bls.png)
- **Chain of survival elements:**
    - **Prevention and preparedness:**
        - <u>Out-of-hospital</u> - improving health (prevention) and improve public education, emergency telecommunication and access to AED (preparedness)
        - <u>In-hospital</u> - eary recognition and rapid response
    - **Activation of emergency response system:**
        - <u>Out-of-hospital</u> - shouting for nearby help and calling 999
        - <u>In-hospital</u> - calling a code to summon rapid response team
    - **High quality CPR, including early defibrillation** - minal interruptions of CPR and early defibrillation related to good resuscitation outcomes
    - **Advanced resuscitation interventions** - vascular access, medications, ECG, advanced cardiac monitoring by in-hospital high-performance team
    - **Post-cardiac arrest care:**
        - <u>Out-of-hospital</u> - shoulrd receive post-cardiac arrest care even after return of spontaneous circulation (ROSC), including artificial ventilation and BP Mx
        - <u>In-hospital</u> - depending on underlying cause, PCI may be required
    - **Recovery** - e.g. rehabilitation for recovering victims (e.g. esp. for neurological recovery)

  
  ![](img/in_vs_out_hospital_chain_of_survival.png)
- **Key differences in adult and paediatric chain of survival** - differing pathology implicated:
    - Adults - most common cause is cardiac in origin and is sudden, hence directly initiate emergency response system if in out-of-hospital context
    - Children - most common cause is respiratory failure and shock, where appropriate Tx (e.g. removal of foreign body) can be given to avoid circulatory collapse
