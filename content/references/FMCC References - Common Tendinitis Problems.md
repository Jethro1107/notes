# [Patient Education Leaflet for OT of Epicondylitis](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10-poCyqTguF4xjlC_eF6ygiqQPxONgJ4/view?usp=drive_link)

- **Role of occupational therapy:**
	- **Improvement of posture:**
		- Holding tote bag - hold with shoulder rather than wrist
		- Wringing towel - use easily dried towels or smaller towels to minimise use of wrist extensors and flexors
		- Using light kitchenware - e.g. avoid using heavy pots or woks (or replacing frying with steaming)
		- Holding kettle  - do not lift kettle, rather support kettle on the desk surface
		- Using a mouse - rest the wrist on the table rather than suspending it mid-air
	- **Use of orthotics** - place the thickened pad over the tender area

- **Physical therapy:**
	- _Acute phase_ - hot therapy followed by cold therapy for 15 minutes x 2-3x a day
	- _Chronic phase_ - hot therapy for 15 minutes x 1-2x a day
