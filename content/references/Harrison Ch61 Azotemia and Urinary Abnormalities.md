# Overview

- **Principles of normal kidney function:**
    - <u>Sufficient renal mass</u> - sufficient number of functional nephrons to maintain body homeostasis
    - <u>Intact cellular processes</u> - sufficient cellular processes involving glomerular filtration, tubular secretion, and tubular reabsorption
- **Clinical manifestations of renal pathological processes:**
    - <u>Azotemia</u> - reduction of glomerular filtration rates
    - <u>Abnormal urinary sediments</u> - presence of RBCs, WBCs, casts, and crystals
    - <u>Proteinuria</u> - abnormal excretion of serum protein
    - <u>Disturbances of urine volume</u> - from polyuria to oliguria/anuria
    - <u>Fluid retention and expansion ot total body fluid volume</u> - manifestation as oedema and hypertension
    - <u>Electrolyte abnormalities</u> - various electrolyte and acid-base disturbances dependent on part of tubule affected

# Azotemia

- **Measurement of renal function:**
    - Plasma creatinine
    - Excretion of freely filtered radioactive isotope that is neither reabsorbed nor secreted
    - Timed collection for creatinine clearance (CrCl)
    - Estimated glomerular filtration rate by formulas
- **Creatinine clearance** - approximation of GFR by measure of plasma and urinary excretion rate for a defined period (24h):
    - <u>Procedure</u> - requires 24h urine collection
    - <u>Rationale</u> - good reflection of renal function as:
        - Freely filtered solute
        - Not reabsorbed by the renal tubules (cf urea)
        - Proximal tubular secretion through organic cation pathway (esp. in chronic kidney disease)
    - <u>Limitations</u>:
        - Dietary ingestion of cooked meat
        - Muscle mass
        - Proximal creatinine excretion in advanced CKD (results in overestimation of GFR)
- **Formula for assessment of renal function from spot SCr:**
    - <u>Cockcroft-Gault</u> - based on age, weight, sex, SCr
    - <u>MOdification of diet in renal disease</u> (4-variable formula) - age, sex, race, SCr
    - <u>CKD-EPI</u> - sex, race, SCr
- **Cystatin C** - more Sn for mild GFR reduction but still influenced by patient's age, race, sex, and is a/w DM, smoking, markers of inflammation
