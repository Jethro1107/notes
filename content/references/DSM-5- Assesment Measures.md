# DSM5: Assessment Measures

## Cross-Cutting Symptom Measures

## Clinican-Rated Dimensions of Psychosis Symptom Severity


![](../img/clinician_rated_dimensions_of_psychotic_disorder_severity.png)

- **Principles:**
    - 8-item measure completed by clinician at the time of clinical assessment
    - Rating the severity of each symptom as experienced by the indiviudal during the past 7d
- **Roles:**
    - Dimensionality approach allows capturing of meaningful variation in severity of symptoms guding treatment planning and prognostic decision-making
    - ALso used in research oon pathophysiological mechanisms of each dimension
    - Other than psychotic symptoms (which reflects disease activity), cognitive impairment also documented to predict functional abilities, as well as mood symptoms, which has prognostic implications and guides Tx
- **Frequency of use** - used as a measure of individual symptom severity over time:
    - May be completed at regular intervals as clinically indicated, depending on stability of individual symptoms and treatment status
    - Consistently high scores on a particular domains may warrant further assessment, treatment and follow-up
