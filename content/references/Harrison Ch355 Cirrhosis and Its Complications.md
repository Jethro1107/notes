### Acute-on-Chronic Liver Failure

- **Definition** - recently recognised clinical syndrome characterised by acutely decomepnsating cirrhosis w/ associated failure of \>=1 organ systems, including liver, kidneys, brain, lung, circulatory system, and coagulation
- **Precipitants of ACLF:**
    - <u>Direct liver injury</u> - alcoholic hepatitis (most common), viral hepatitis flares, autoimmune hepatitis, drug-induced liver injury
    - <u>Systemic effects</u> - infection (SBP, other bacterial or fungal infections), GI bleeds, post-operative state
- **Pathophysiology of ACLF:**
    - <u>Precipitant</u> - as mentioned above
    - <u>Systemic inflammatory response syndrome</u> - sepsis-like response to the initial insult followed by multi-organ failure
- **Mx:**
    - <u>Search and Mx of precipitating causes</u> - screen for infections, GI bleeds etc.
    - <u>ICU care</u> - supportive care for multi-organ failure
    - <u>Mx of complications of cirrhosis</u> - may arise w/ during the hospitalisation
    - <u>Urgent referral for LT</u> - as definitive Mx
- **Prognosis** - extremely poor:
    - 28 day mortality 20-70%
    - Increased risk w/ number of organ involvement

### Liver-Lung Syndromes

- **Hepatopulmonary syndrome** (HPS):
    - **Definition** - ventilation-perfusion mismatch in a patient w/ cirrhosis w/o significant lung disease, resulting in arterial hypoxaemia
    - **Epidemiology** - relatively common complication of cirrhosis (4-32%)
    - **Pathophysiology of HPS:**
        - <u>Intrapulmonary vascular dilatation</u> - results in intra-pulmonary shunting, usually in the base of the lungs (small V/Q) and VQ mismatch
    - **Clinical features of HPS:**
        - <u>Dyspnoea</u> - likely as a result of hypoxaemia
        - <u>Platypnoea-orthodeoxia</u> - paradoxical SOB when upright, relieved w/ supine position due to gravitational effect on blood flow (i.e. increased shunting when in upright position)
    - **Dx** - demonstration of hypoxaemia w/o evidence of significant lung disease and shunt on bubble echocardiography
    - **Mx:**
        - O2 supplementation (may not fully respond w/ large shunts)
        - Liver transplantation
- **Portopulmonary hypertension** (PPHT):
    - **Definition** - pulmonary HTN in a patient w/ portal HTN
    - **Epidemiology** - rare complication of cirrhosis (\< 5% of decompensated cirrhosis)
    - **Pathophysiology of PPHT** - production of vasoconstrictor in response to portal HTN affeecting the pulmonary artery
    - **Clinical features of PPHT:**
        - Usually asymptomatic esp. in early disease
        - Shortness of breath on exertion w/ fatigue
    - **Dx** - right heart cathterisation
    - **Mx:**
        - Vasodilatory medications
        - Liver transplantation
