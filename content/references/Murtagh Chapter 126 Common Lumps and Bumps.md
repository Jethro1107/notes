## Diagnostic Approach to Lumps and Bumps

- **Physical examination of the lump and bump:**
	- _Inspection_ - number, site, morphology, size (two-dimensions)
	- _Palpation_:
		- Consistency
		- Tenderness
		- Mobility
		- Fluctuations
		- Attachments (to overlying skin, and other anatomical structures)
		- Impulse
		- Thrills
	- _Other special examinations_:
		- Auscultation - bruit
		- Transillumination - contents of the lump and bump
- **Description of a lump and bump:**
	- **Description on visual inspection:**
		- Number
		- Site
		- Shape (regular vs irregular)
		- Size (two-dimensional in metric units)
		- Colour
	- **Description on palpation:**
		- Consistency - soft, firm, hard
		- Contents - solid, cystic, fluctuant (fluid-filled)
		- Mobility - mobile, immobile
		- Attachment - to overlying skin and underlying anatomical structures
	- **Special features:**
		- Pulsation - transmitted or direct
		- Transillumination - - transilluminant, non-transilluminant
		- Auscultation - bruit
		- Reducibility
		- Colour and temperature
- **Relations of the lump to anatomical structures:**
	- _In the skin_ - lump moves w/ movement of the overlying skin and overlying skin not pinchable (e.g. sebaceous cyst)
	- _In the subcutaneous tissue_ - overlying skin can be moved/ pinched over the lump (+ve slipping sign: the lump felt to be moved when pressed on the edge)
	- _Attached to/ in the muscle_ - mobile when muscle relaxed, but reduced moblity on contraction of the muscle
	- _Arising from tendon or joint_ - change in mobility/ shape of the lump on movement of the tendon/ joint
	- _In the bone_ - immobile and best outline w/ the muscle relaxed
## Lumps of the Skin and Mucous Membranes

### Fibroepithelial Polyps

- **Definition** - benign skin overgrowths, w/ no malignant potential
- **Other terminologies:**
	- Skin tags
	- Acrochordon
	- Benign squamous papilloma
	- Soft fibroma
- **Epidemiology:**
	- Increasing incidence w/ age
	- Most commonly found on the neck, axillae, groin, and trunk
- **Clinical features of fibroepithelial polyps:**
	- Benign skin overgrowths w/ pedunculated base
	- Not a/w any pain/ tenderness
	- May be irritating or unsightly to the patient
- **Mx:**
	- **Principles of Mx** - patient's preference of:
		- Leaving in situ
		- Removal
	- **Methods of removal:**
		- Removal w/ scissors or bone forceps
		- Removal w/ diathermy or electrocautery of base
		- Removal by forceps after application of liquid nitrogen

### Epidermoid (Senaceous) Cysts 

- **Definition** - cystic growth in the skin
- **Other terminologies:**
	- Pilar cysts
	- Keratinous cysts
	- Wens
	- Epidermoid cysts
	- Sebaceous cysts
- **Clinical associations** - Gardner syndrome:
	- Onset in puberty
	- A variant form of Familial adenomatous polyposis
- **Clinical features of epidermoid cysts:**
	- A regular, round lump
	- Central punctum containing keratin
	- May be erythematous and tender if inflammed
	- Soft to firm consistency
	- Fluctuant, as filled w/ sebaceous material
	- Mobile
	- Attached to the skin
	- ![[Pasted image 20260524175911.png]]
- **Mx:**
	- **Principles of Mx** - patient's preference of:
		- Leaving in situ
		- Removal
	- **Methods of removal:**
		- _Incision into cyst_ - incision into cyst to bisect it, squeeze the contents out
		- _Incision over the cyst and blunt dissection_ - separate skin from the cyst by blunt dissection, then apply digital pressure to cause it to pop out
		- _Standard dissection_ - ellipse incision containing the punctum followed by removal of the cysts for histopathological confirmation

### Warts and Papilloma

- **Definition** - benign skin tumours caused by the human papillomavirus
- **Pathophysiology of warts and papillomas:**
	- _Viral entry_ - invasion of skin through small abrasions, via direct/ formite contract (potential of auto-inocculation to other body areas)
	- _Skin growth_ - abnormal skin growth in different configurations
- **Natural Hx of warts and papilloma:**
	- Average incupation period of ~ 4 mo
	- About 25% resolve spontaneously in 6 mo and 70% in 2y
- **Classsification of warts** - most are skin coloured:
	- ![[Pasted image 20260524180628.png]]
	- _Common warts_ - skin-coloured tumours w/ rough surface mainly on fingers, elbows, and knees
	- _Plane warts_ - skin-coloured flat elevations occuring in linear clusters along the scratch line, prone to Koebner phenomenon
	- _Filiform warts_ - fine, elongated warts, usually in the face or neck
	- _Digitate warts_ - finger-like projections, usually on the scalp
- **Mx:**
	- **Principles of Mx** - preferred Tx modality dependent on type of warts:
		- _Common warts_ - topical therapy +/- cryotherapy
		- _Filiform and digitate warts_ - liquid nitrogen or electrodissection
		- _Genital warts_ - podophyllotoxin 0.5% paint or imiqumod
