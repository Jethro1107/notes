# Part 4: Automated External Defibrillator

- **Defibrillation** - coordinated electrical shock to restore sinus rhythm and return of circullation
- **Early defibrillation** - evidence points to increased survival from cardiac arrest caused by two shockable rhythms (pVT and VF), hence AED is used to assess rhythm immediately after AED arrives
- **Universal steps to operating an AED:**
    1.  Open case and power on
    2.  Attach AED pads on <u>bare chest</u> - anterolateral vs anteroposterior attachment 
    ![](img/aed_pad_position.png)
    3.  Attach electrodes to device
    4.  Clear the victim and analyse the rhythm (some devices require pressing the button)
    5.  Shock the victim if AED shows shockable rhythm (ensure no one is in contact with victim and press the shock button)
    6.  Immediately resume CPR if shock delivered or not shockable rhythm
    7.  Repeat step 3-6 every 5 cycles (i.e. 2 minutes), which will be prompted by AED
