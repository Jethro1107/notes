# On Dreams

- **Two views of dreams:**
    - <u>Popular view</u> (optimistic view) - dreams driven by psychical phenomenon and are hypothesised to have meaning
    - <u>Scientific view</u> (pessimistic view) - unintelligible nature of dreams reflect nervous processes (i.e. cortical elements) in response to a change in the internal environment
- **Meanings of dreams** - two definitions:
    - <u>Psychical significance</u> - dreams perhaps reflecting psychical processes which may have an underlying biological significance (e.g. survival)
    - <u>Personal significance</u> - to draw sense of a particular dream
- **Freud's argument for the popular view of dreams** - dreams are manifestations of psychical processes which can be described as:
    - <u>Evidence from psychoanalysis</u> - approaching dreams through psychoanalysis in a similar manners to correlate psychopathology to psychical contents yields meaningful results
- **Reason for scientific view:**
    - <u>Suppression</u> - reflexic self-critism when prompted to search for a deeper meaning which may reflect a defense barrier
    - <u>Unintelligible</u> - primarily because the dream content that manifests has been distorted through transformation from the latent dream content
- **Terminology of dream content/ thought** - manifest contents transformed into latent content through psychical processes:
    - <u>Manifest content</u> - the dream as it appears, the story that the dreamer remembers upon waking
    - <u>Latent content</u> - pure untransformed psychical content with a hidden unconscious meaning
    - <u>Dream work</u> - Freud's terminology for the overall psychical process of transformation of latent content (dream thought/ idea) to manifest content (dream content)
    - <u>Dream analysis</u> - Freud's terminology for decoding latent content from manifest content through psychoanalysis
- **If dreams were formed in such a manner, two questions arise:**
    - 1\. Can the way transformation of latent into manifest content be systematised and abstracted (i.e. what are the psychical processes involved)?
    - 2\. Why is it necessary for the such transformation
- **Forms of dream work:**
    - Dramatisation
    - Condensation
    - Displacement
