# GC Cancer Block Week 1: Hallmarks of Cancer <span class="tag" tag-name="Lecture"><span class="smallcaps">Lecture</span></span>

- **Hallmark of cancers** - common characteristics shared by cancer (read robbins again):
    - Hanahan and Weinberg - 2000 (6 core hallmarks), 2010 (10 core hallmarks), 2022 (14 core hallmarks)
    - Self-sufficiency in growth signals
    - Insensitivity to anti-growth signals
    - Evading apoptosis (immortality)
    - Limitless replicative potential
    - Sustained angiogenesis
    - Tissue invasion and metastasis
- Limitless replicative potential:
    - Telomeres - repeted sequences at end of chromosome, which are hard to replicate, such that it progressively shorten with cell division 
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/telomere_attrition_during_cell_division.png)
        - Difficult to replicate because insufficient primers to bind to the end of chromosomes during dna replication??? –\> telomerase allow extension to extend telomere with RNA template
    - Cancer cells **upregulate telomerase** (2009 nobel)
- **Sustained angiogenesis:**
    - Balance between proliferation and necrosis dependent on O2 supply and demand
    - Necrotic core on H&E stain
    - Hypoxic response pathway - HIF1-alpha (nobel 2019):
        - Normoxia - HIF1-alha will be hydroxylase, degraded by VHL enzyme through protein ubiquitination
        - Hypoxia - HIF-alpha cannot be hydroxylated (no O), translocated to HRE to increase transcription of HIF-target genes (e.g. VEGF)

    
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/hif_1_in_angiogenesis.png)
    - Cancer - inactivate VHL, activate HIF, secret VEGF
- **Invasion and metastasis:**
    - Invasion through basement membrane, then into lymphatics and blood vessels
    - Modes of metastasis:
        - Lung is common metastatic sites simply because through lymphatics (return to right heart through thoracic duct)
        - Transcoelomic spread (intra-abdominal cancers)
- Additional hallmarks:
    - Deregulating cellular genetics - Warburg effect (read up) to preferentially choose aerobic glycolysis (shifts focus from maintaing cellular function by ATP to proliferation, i.e. require more amino acid and nucleotide synthesis) 
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/dysregulation_of_cellular_energetics.png)
        - Read role of PEP, IDH1-2, Myc which are often involved in cancer biology
    - Immune evasion:
        - T cell inhibition (e.g. PD-L1)
        - Underlies role of immunotherapy
    - Genome instability - chromosome abnormalities, DNA mismatch replair pathways (MMR, BRCA) results in multiclonal 
    ![](~/Documents/notes/img/genomic_instability_in_cancer_roles.png)
    - Tumour promoting inflammation
    - Unlocking phenotypic plasticity - blocking differentiation, de-differentiation, transdifferentiation
    - Non-mutational epigenetic reprogramming
    - Senescent cells
    - Polymorphic microbiomes

# GC Cancer Block Week 1: Principles of Screening <span class="tag" tag-name="Lecture"><span class="smallcaps">Lecture</span></span>

- **Definition** - the presumptive identification of unrecognized disease or defect by the application of tests, examinations, or other procedures which could be applied rapidly. Screening tests sort out apparently well persons who probably have a disease from those who probably do not, and is not intended to be diagnostic.
- **Goals:**
    - Reduced mortality/ morbidity
    - Reduce disease burden for the population
    - Provide cost-effective screening to target population
