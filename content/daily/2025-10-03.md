## Clinical Syndromes associated with defects of the innate immune system

- **Complement defects:**
    - <u>Classical pathway</u> - a/w encapsulated bacteria infection
    - <u>Mannose binding lectin pathway</u> - a/w sinopulmonary infections and Neisseria meningitidis
    - <u>Aternative pathway</u> - a/w Neisseria meningitidis
    - <u>Late complement deficiency</u> (C5-9) - recurrent Neisseria meningitidis infections
- **Neutrophil defects:**
    - **Common neutrophil defects:**
        - <u>Quantitiative defects</u> - e.g. neutropenia (e.g. chemotherapy, haematological malignancy)
        - <u>Qualitative defects</u> - chronic granulomatous disease, Leukocyte adhesion deficiency syndromes
    - **Predisposing infections:**
        - <u>Bacterial infections</u> - G+ cocci (Staphyloccocal, Streptococcal, Enterococcal infections), and aerobic G- rods (E. coli, P. aeruginosa)
        - <u>Fungal infections</u> - e.g. candida infections, invasive pulmonary aspergillosis
- **Chronic granulomaous disease:**
    - <u>Mode of inheritence</u> - usually X-linked
    - <u>Pathophysiology</u>:
        - Abnormal respiratory burst oxidase activity
        - Results in defective intracellular killing
    - <u>Susceptible infections</u> - a/w organisms w/ high catalase activity:
        - S. aureus
        - Serratia marcescens
        - Burkholderia cepaicia
        - Chromobacterium violaceum
        - Nocardia
        - Aspergillus
- **Leukocyte adhesion deficiency syndrome:**
    - <u>Pathophysiology</u>:
        - Impaired adhesion of neutrophils to endothelial cells
        - Inability to migrate into site of tissue damage
    - <u>Susceptible infections</u>:
        - Staphylococcus
        - Pseudomonas aeruginosa
- **Autoantibody against interferon-gamma:**
    - <u>Epidemiology</u> - adult onset in Asians
    - <u>Pathophysiology</u> - Ab against INF-gamma:
        - Prevents activation of macrophages to kill intracellular microbes
        - Persistence of intracellular microbes
    - <u>Associated infections</u> - obligate intracellular organisms:
        - Mycobacterium - e.g. MTB, NTM
        - Non-typhoidal salmonella
        - Burkholderia pseudomallei
        - Penicillium marneffei
        - VZV
        - Crytococcosis, histioplasmosis

## Systemic Conditions associated with defects of the adaptive immune system

- **Systemic conditions a/w decreased immunity:**
    - Breaks in physical barriers
    - DM
    - Cirrhosis
    - Renal failure
    - Autoimmune disease
    - Maliginancy
    - Transplantation
    - Iron overload
    - Splenectomy
    - Drugs (e.g. steroids)
    - Malnutrition
    - Pregnancy
    - Extremes of age
- **Charaterisation of the immunocomprimised host** - see [[GC101 Diagnosis of infections]]:
    - HIV vs Non-HIV
    - Transplant vs Non-transplant
    - BMT vs Solid organ transplant
    - Malignancy vs non-malignancy
    - Haematological malignancy vs solid organ tumour
    - Chronic illness (DM, cirrhosis), autoimmune disease (e.g. SLE), splenectomy
- **Splenectomy:**
    - <u>Immune function of spleen</u> - Ab production esp. against encapsulated bacteria (due to polysaccharide Ag recognition)
    - <u>Susceptible organisms</u>:
        - S. pneumoniae - e.g. sinopulmonary infections, meningitis, septicaemia
        - H. influenzae B - sinopulmonary infections, meningitis
        - N. meningiditis - meningitis, meningococcal septicaemia
        - Capnocytophaga canimorsus
    - <u>Prevention</u>:
        - Vaccination - Pneumococcal vacine, influenza vaccine +/- H. influenzae type B vaccine, meningococcal vaccine
        - ABx prophylaxis (ideally life-long)
