# Johnathan Bi - Girard's Mimetic Desire Explained Lecture 2

- **Girardian psychology** - tripartite narrowing down of human behaviour, to mimetic behaviour, to mimetic desire, to metaphysical desire
    - **Part of the system** - part of the whole system driving human behaviour, but differentiates humans from animals, and powers historical events
- **Mimesis** - humans are naturally social creatures prone to co-dependence (i.e. Hume's violin string analogy)
    - Analogous to Hume's Treaties of Human Nature on Sympathy - As in strings equally wound up, the motions of one communicates itself to the rest; so all the affections readily pass from one person to another, and beget correspondent movements in every human creature (copying of emotions from one human to another)
    - Mimesis - tendancy and capacity of copying of all sorts of behaviours from one human to another
    - Evidence for mimesis - hermeneutical proof (i.e. an a priori hypothesis and see how much phe\`\`nomenons that can be explained) vs empirical evidence (Andrew Meltzov experiment on infants, concept of 'mirror neurons')
    - Significance of mimesis:
        - Authority that provides normative certainty (i.e. concept of recognition, prestige, sacred/apotheosis) - law of induction? if the internalised values from others are subsequently are reflected in the Other, then we feel validated normative value of an object dependent on normative value of others ingested and digested through mimesis
            - i.e. Value of object is determined by the internalisation of value systems of Others
        - Other authorities of certainty - e.g. expierence (induction), reason (logic), but these seems to be out-powered by mimesis
- **Mimetic desire** - desires ignited by observations of the mediator desiring a particular object:
    - <u>Metaphysical desire</u> - desire to be (what objects say about me, i.e. coquette)
    - <u>Physical desire</u> - desire to experience (what is experienced regarding the quality of the object)
    - Utility vs identity
    - All instances of mimetic desire have both aims
- **Metaphysical desire** - desire to be (i.e. identity, ego, self-conception, spirit), a form of prideful snobbism
    - Authentic desire:
        - Romantic view of desire - strength correlated with authenticity (but in novelistic view –\> strength of desire comes from desire to be, that is vanity, not authenticity)
        - Authentic desire in terms of girard - requires a strong physical desire, and to be authentic, required to calm metaphysical desires
    - Any vanity/ snobbism is a dead givaway of the amaeturity in the field –\> because the desire is purely metaphysical simply because they have no understanding of the actual experience
    - i.e. all desire is a desire for being is a misnomer of a title –\> Girard simply thinks that mimetic desire is a spectrum
    - Physical desires is also mimetic in nature even if directed to the object - because the interpretation of our experience is also based on normative values where certainty can only be achieved through the collective (often unaminous belief)
- **Abstraction of the desire for being** - to exist in Great Measure (an egotistic view, seeing yourself as capable of the divine):
    - **Glory** - to achieve recognition, prestige, sacridity among social circle (e.g. Social Media Presence, IG vs BeReal)
    - **Immortality** - social perception of your identity persists throughout eternity (cf Crime and Punishment Raskolnikov's justification of his crimes)
    - **Power** - great effect among the social stratosphere
- This is a pessimistic psychology - we are motivated by an ideal that we cannot be achieved
- **Original sin:**
    - <u>Thomas Aquinas</u> - Pride is the origin Sin (i.e. Satan's sin to covet God's position)
        - Because metaphysical desire stems from pride –\> Metaphysical desire is the original sin
    - <u>Cf Genesis</u> - triangular desire depicted by Adam and Eve (subject), God (mediator), forbidden apple (object)
        - <u>Despicability</u> - they realise they were naked (i.e. difference with ideal being is subsequently enlarged)
- **Nature of metaphysical desire:**
    - **Malleable**
    - **Powerful**
    - **Deceitful**
    - **Unpredictable**
