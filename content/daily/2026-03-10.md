# Reflections on Case Clerking and Psychiatry Exposure on 2026-03-10

- **Key discussion with GPT 5.4 on perplexity:**
    - Describes main problem with basic psychiatric education, want to self study more phenomenological, philosophical, psychoanalytical approaches for formulations
    - Referenced APSA reading list and analyze needs for deeper readings
    - The goal is to understand what is happening in the patient’s world and to become more empathic without becoming vague, diagnostically impartial
    - To not be purely reductionalist, but also not romantically anti-diagnostic
    - <https://www.perplexity.ai/search/https-apsa-org-wp-content-uplo-_cwGMxhOSiuhCOYiYn_iDw>
- The best ideas and thinking always arises at a place where documentation is impossible, i.e. being in the shower.
- Seen one long case in clerking, which is a case of adjustment disorder and suicidality, two other brief intervirews of chronic schizophrenia in ?partial remission
- Major problems in Hx taking:
    - Circumstantial talking, time wasting
    - Putting words into patients mouth
    - language, metaphor, syntax, framing, omission, and tone, even though these features can change formulation and understanding
    - Subjectivity in interpretation of whether fits the diagnostic criteria
    - Balancing between diagnosis and comprehensive Hx taking vs "therapeutic alliance"
    - recognition-by-identification vs recognition-as-acknowledgement of the other person’s singularity
- Gripe with descriptive psychopathology, admittedly there is good and bad:
    - Atheoretical
    - Promotes checkbox driven approach rather than mechanism/ psychopathology
    - Cross-sectional approach… i.e. at this time point does the patient fit criteria for illness X, whereas clinical practice likely requires horizontal assessment
    - Appears less empathetic… the DSM-5 provides as a baseline for justifying a diagnosis, not the pathway for making the diagnosis. I can imagine that the tactful Hx and MSE should be driven by interpretation rather than checkboxes
    - It is possible that the underlying phenomenology endured reflects more on the nature of the psychopathology better classification
    - However, data-driven, key information regarding epidemiology, clinical course, prognosis, and major causes
- Psychodynamics, psychoanalysis and philosophy ([[Approach to Psychopathology]]):
    - Limitations in psychoanalysis, over-interpretation in an amaeteur, trying to derive meaning where meaning does not exist
    - My philosophical/ existential views do not necessarily matches that of patients, i.e. that of the problem of transference and counter-transference
    - Formulations only reflect the best hypothesis, best understanding dependent on rapport, time available… but then does etiology always have an influence on management?
    - Misinterpretation as a form of violence: it may derive meanings too quickly, confuse your worldview with the patient’s, and mistake a provisional formulation for truth.
    - Still assumption driven. A lot of assumptions are made… ?does not account for human impuslivity, transcultural factors
    - Even if we were able to transfer our understanding to the patient (impart the underlying "meaning" to the patient), the path to recovery remains labourous… the work of mourning/ melancholia ([[Freud - 1917 - Mourning and Melancholia]]) cannot be skipped even if one understands the procss with clarity (i.e. the shadow of the object thus MUST fell upon the ego IRRESPECTIVE OF CONSCIOUS UNDERSTANDING)
    - understanding is not the same as cure; better language and better recognition may humanize care, but they do not magically bypass grief, conflict, chronicity, or the slow work of recovery
- The question stands in finding a balance:
    - How to be diagnostically accurate without flattening patients into a checklist
    - How to seek meaning without being overly intrusive and interpretive
