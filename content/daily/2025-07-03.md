## Miscarriage

- **Definition:**
    - <u>Clinical definition</u> - pregnancy loss at \< 24 weeks
    - <u>WHO definition</u> - expulsion from its mother of an embryo or fetus \<= 500g, corresponding to gestational age of up to 20 complete weeks of congestion with <u>no signs of life</u>
- **Terminology** - form of miscarriage defined by clinical presentation:
    - <u>Threatened miscarriage</u> - bleeding from the uterus prior to 24 weeks, with the cervix not dilated and the fetus alive
    - <u>Inevitable miscarriage</u> - bleeding from the uterus prior to 24 weeks with pain and dilatation of the cervix
    - <u>Incomplete miscarriage</u> - clinical evidence that part of the conceptus has been expelled, but presence of retained tissues suggested by continuous bleeding
    - <u>Complete miscarriage</u> - the whole conceptus has been expelled
    - <u>Recurrent miscarriage</u> - \>=3 consecutive miscarriages (\>= 2 by ESHRE)
    - <u>Silent miscarriage</u> - pregnancy failure identified before the expulsion of fetal and placenteal tissues
- **Salient points of Hx** - suspicion raised in female of reproductive raised presenting w/ 1) acute abdomen, and 2) PV bleeding:
    - **Chance of pregnancy** - requires added confirmation from pregnancy test
    - **Menstrual Hx:**
        - <u>LMP</u> - note recent LMP does not r/o pregnancy as the uterine bleeding from miscarriage may be mistakened as menstruation (ask about the <u>previous period before LMP</u>)
    - **Associated Sx** - note timing:
        - Abdominal pain - note quality and duration
        - Vaginal bleeding - note quantity and severity
        - Passage of tissue mass - send to pathology to differentiate from decidua
    - **If known pregnancy** - ask if:
        - <u>Planned</u> - if unplanned, requires educating on contraception
- **P/E:**
    - **Primary assessment and general examination** - haemodynamic status, pallor, and general condition
    - **Abdominal examination** - look for abdominal tenderness (points towards EP)
    - **Pelvic examination:**
        - <u>Examination of introitus</u> - presence of blood stain, tissue mass
        - <u>Examination of cervix</u> - determine if os opened or closed to determine type of pregnancy:
            - Cervix dilated - inevitable miscarriage
            - Cervix nont-dilated - threatened miscarriage
        - <u>Bimanual examination</u> - determine whether uterine size is corresponding to gestational age

- **Ix:**
    - **Pregnancy test** - r/o pregnancy complication
    - **Bloods** - CBC, T&S, Rh status:
        - <u>CBC</u> - Hb and MCV
        - <u>T&S</u> - transfusion
        - <u>Rh status</u> - identify Rh -ve patients who may need anti-D IG
    - **Pelvic USG** - determine the type of miscarriage:
        - <u>Incomplete miscarriage</u> - thick, irregular heterogenous echos in midline of uterine cavity, where maximum AP diameter of \<= 15 mm is suggestive of retained products
        - <u>Complete miscarriage</u> - well-defined regular endometrial line (consider EP especially if symptomatic)
        - <u>Silent miscarriage</u> - gestational sac diameter \>= 25 mm w/ no evidence of embryoo or yolk sac; CRL \>= 7mm w/ no evidence of cardiac pulsation (requires repeat scan 7d to confirm Dx)
    - **Pathological examination** - pathological tissue mass
- **USG milestones:**
    - 3-4 weeks - endometrium and decidua
    - 4-5 weeks - gestational sacs
    - 5 weeks - yolk sac and embryonic pole
    - 5-6 weeks - embryonic cardiac activity
- Read up NICE guideline on miscarriage (read up sonographic definition of different types of miscarriage as well)
- **Mx:**
    - **Principles of Mx:**
        - <u>Treatment options</u>:
            - Expectant Mx
            - Medical Mx
            - Surgical Mx
        - <u>Additional care points</u> - pain Mx, anti-D immunoglobulin, and patient counselling
- **Mx:**
    - Threatened miscarriage - obsevation or vaginal micronised progesterone in women who had previous 1st trimester miscarriage and continued til 15 completed weeks of gestation (note no evidence regarding women w/ no prior miscarriage)
    - Silent, incomplete - expectant Mx for 1-2 weeks with misoprostol/ suction evacuation
    - Inevitable - expectant Mx, suction evaculation
    - Complete - r/o ectopic pregnancy
- Patient counselling:
    - No evidence show requiring waiting a year before next pregnancy after a miscarriage - not a/w adverse pregnancy outcomes
- Approach to recurrent miscarriage:
    - Ix:
        - Antiphospholipid antibodies
        - Thyroid function
        - Karyotyping
        - Screening for uterine malformations
        - Perform thrombophilia screen if second trimester miscarriage

## Ectopic pregnancy

- **Clinical presentation:**
    - Classical triad of missed period, vaginal bleeding, abdominal pain
    - Shock and syncope
    - Shoulder pain (referred)
    - Abdominal tenderness with varying degree of peritonism (including cervical excitation)
- **Dx** - can be a clinical diagnosis
- **Risk factors of ectopic pregnancy** - doesn't seem to be complete?
    - Previous ectopic pregnancy
    - Previous Hx of STD (esp. chlamydia)
    - Tubal damage from salpingotomy
    - Hx of infertility
    - Assisted reprodction techniques
    - Increasing age
    - Smoking
- **Ix:**
    - CBC, Rh status, T&S
    - Urine pregnancy test, serum hcg
    - Pelvic ultrasound
        - Sliding sign (diagnostic) - adnexal mass moving separately to the ovary + visible yolk sac or fetal pole
        - Bagel sign (high probability) - adnexal mass moving separately to the ovary w/0 gestational sac
        - Possible - collection of fluid within uterine cavity and empty uterus (pseudosac)
        - Haemoperitoneum
- **Mx** - read algo in slides:
    - General:
        - Fasting
        - Large bore IV access for fluid resuscitation
        - Close observation
    - If Sx of haemoperitoneum and haemodynamically unstable - emergency diagnistic laparoscopy +/- salpingectomy/ salpingotomy
- Read up in HK EPAC in streamlining Mx
