# PFA

- Psychological first aid
    - No psychotherapy required
    - Generalised to be performed by anyone
    - National Child Traumatic Stress Network (National Center for PTSD)
    - CISM? More difficult?
- Five empirically-supported early interventional principles:
    - Safety
    - Calming
    - Connectedness
    - Self- & Community-Efficacy
    - Hope
- Bring:
    - Water
    - Jacket/ warm clothing
    - Essentials
    - Charger
    - Some snacks
    - Tissue/ wet tissue
    - Mask and alcohol
- Wear comfy clothes - consider plain colour
- Core actions:
    - Contact and engagement
    - Safety and comfort
    - Stabilization
    - Information gathering
    - Practical Assistance
    - Connection with social supports
    - Information on coping
    - Linkage with collaborative services
