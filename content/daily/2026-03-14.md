- **Reflections on a case in rehab round** - 29/F, tertiary education level (sociology major), prior stable occupation, known case of schizoaffective disorder ([[Schizophrenia]]), expressed interest in being a household security after recovery from 2nd psychotic relapse:
    - Main themes - 1) life worlds of schizophrenia, 2) unappreciated capacity/ bandwidth to overcome information overload, 3) AI-driven studying
    - Why sould someone with such good premorbid predictors of social functioning willingly choose to just be a security officer.
    - Nothing wrong with that, but appears to be giving up to early, as only 29y, and premorbid probably predicts capable of doing more complex work.
    - ?Mundane job as safe space to prevent psychotic relapse due to minimal stimulation and thinking, thus is the <u>life-world of a schizophrenic</u> ([[Oxford Handbook of Phenomenological Psychopathology Ch1- Introduction]]), one might be forced to hide from stimulation
    - Makes me think of the degree of information overload I face, that had driven me to take an approach of AI-driven literature review and synthesis to cope with this.
    - I now realise I had been under-appreciating the bandwidth to not be completely overwhelmed by various sources of information, that I have the capacity to actively seek information and stimulus (when she has to hide from it)
    - Unrelated to her, as I am thinking for myself here… that my search for AI-driven workflows here signifies the distress of information overload… but rather this likely represents a protective rather than a malprotective change… I wonder if we can apply this cognitively to them
    - And thinking tangentially, is there a thematic connection between hallucination by LLMs, and the idea that excessive stimulation in schizophrenia patients as precipitating factor of psychotic relapse
