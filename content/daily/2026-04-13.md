## Consultation models

- **Consultation** - the crucible:
	- J Spence 1976...
	- David Pendelton
- **2 modes of consultation**: Task-oriented, doctor-centred consultation vs behaviour-oriented patient centred... the fact is that doctor centred predominates, only when doctor centre fails transition into patient-centre:
	- Byrne and long (describes form) / Stott and Davis (doctors drive content)... all driven by doctors
	- Six category intervention analysis, Helen's folk model is a more comprehensive version of ICE
- Level of care:  patient centred service orientation (most basic) vs Patient centred consultation vs patient representative vs patient in charge
- **Diagnostic models** - major difference in Ix rates (e.g. 5% Ix rate in primary care):
	- Induction method (hospitalist)
	- Murtaugh diagnostic model hypothetical deductive method 
- Super open ended can literally eliminate half the differential diagnosis 1 minute and the patient will tell you the likely diagnosis
